FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

The things people call us about most. If yours isn't here, call (301) 252-3586 — we'll answer.

How fast can I get a dumpster?
Most addresses in our core service area (Beltsville, Greenbelt, College Park, Laurel, Hyattsville) can be delivered the same day if you call before noon. Outside that radius, next-day is standard.
What size dumpster do I need?
10 yard for garage cleanouts, 15 for single-room remodels, 20 for whole-house cleanouts (our most popular), 25 for additions, and 30 for full demolitions or large construction. Call us — we'll help you pick the right one.
How much does it cost?
Flat-rate pricing depends on size and ZIP code. Typical 20-yard rentals in our core area run around $450–$525 for a 7-day rental with tonnage included. Every quote is in writing — what we quote is what you pay.
What can I put in the dumpster?
Most construction, demo, household, and yard debris. We can't take: hazardous waste (paints, solvents, oils), tires, batteries, refrigerators with refrigerant, propane tanks, or asbestos. Ask if you're unsure — we'll point you to the right disposal.
Do I need a permit?
Permits are only needed if the dumpster sits in the street or public right-of-way. Most driveways and private lots are permit-free. Each city handles it differently — we'll help you figure it out for your address.
How long can I keep the dumpster?
Standard rental is 7 days. Need more? Day extensions are inexpensive — just call. Need it gone sooner? Same-day pickup available in most cases.
Will the dumpster damage my driveway?
No. Our drivers lay protective boards under the wheels and corner posts before setting the container down. We've delivered on asphalt, concrete, and pavers without issue. If you have a specialty surface, mention it when you book and we'll plan accordingly.
How heavy can I load it?
Each size includes a generous tonnage allowance: 10yd = 1 ton, 15yd = 1.5 ton, 20yd = 2 ton, 25yd = 2.5 ton, 30yd = 3 ton. Overage is billed per ton at a flat rate we tell you up front. Heavy debris (concrete, brick, dirt) has lower fill limits — call for guidance.
How much space do I need?
For most sizes, allow about 22–24 feet of length and 10 feet of width plus overhead clearance (no low branches or wires). A 30-yard needs a longer run-up. We can walk you through your driveway photos if you're unsure.
Do you offer Spanish-speaking service?
Sí. Hablamos español en la oficina y atendemos solicitudes en español por teléfono y formulario. Llámanos al (301) 252-3586.
How do I pay?
Credit card, debit card, ACH, check, or cash. Commercial accounts can be billed net-15 with credit approval. We email an invoice the day of delivery.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
No problem — call at least 24 hours before delivery to reschedule or cancel without charge. Same-day cancellations may carry a trip fee depending on whether the truck has already been dispatched.
