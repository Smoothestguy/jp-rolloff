The 15-yard measures 16 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall, and it holds roughly seven pickup-truck loads of debris. Pricing is per material: a 15-yard is **priced by what is going in, so call for a quote** (aggregates are available only in the 10-, 15-, and 20-yard sizes). The rental includes delivery, pickup, and a 14-day rental with a 1.5-ton (3,000 lb) weight allowance — extra days are $5/day, extra weight is $105 per additional ton, and pickup is on request, so just call to schedule the haul.

The four-foot wall height is the quiet advantage of this size. ## Which Heather Hills jobs suit the 15-yard

Heather Hills is part of the Levitt-built portion of Belair, which means single-family detached homes on roughly quarter-acre lots set along tree-lined streets. The original models Levitt offered across the development were the Cape Cod, the Rancher, the Colonial, the Country Clubber, and the Manor House, and Heather Hills was among the later sections completed when Belair finished out at roughly 7,500 homes. Six decades on, those mid-century houses are dated by today's standards, right down to their original kitchens and baths — and that drives the one-room, one-system work the 15-yard handles best:

- **Kitchen remodel.** Tearing out the cabinets, countertops, and flooring from one original Levitt kitchen fills a 15-yard about right — cabinets, drywall, old subfloor, and packaging, with a little room to spare. This is the single most common reason a 15 lands on a Heather Hills driveway.
- **Roofing tear-off.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off on one of these detached homes fits comfortably, with the 1.5-ton allowance covering most of these modest mid-century roof footprints. Shingles are heavy, so we will talk through the square footage with you before we drop it.
- **Two-bath gut.** Pulling tile, tubs, vanities, and old plumbing from a pair of bathrooms is a classic 15-yard load — more debris than a 10 wants, not enough to justify a 20. On a 60-year-old Levitt build, that often means dated fixtures and original wall material coming out together.
- **Basement cleanout.** Clearing out decades of accumulation — or the debris from finishing or re-finishing a basement — works well in the 15, since the lower walls make loading from a stairwell easier. The same size handles an estate or downsizing cleanout when a long-held Heather Hills home changes hands.

If your project is bigger — a whole-house interior gut between owners, or a multi-room renovation that runs the length of the house — that may be a 20 or larger instead. We will tell you straight which size your job needs rather than upsell you into a container you cannot fill.

## Placement, permits, and the H-streets south of Route 50

**Driveway placement is the natural fit here, and in Heather Hills there is a real reason for it.** This is the only Belair section with no direct road connection to the rest of the community — US 50 cuts it off, and the link to the rest of Bowie is a pedestrian bridge over the highway, not a road. The neighborhood even got its own elementary school, Heather Hills Elementary, because of that separation. For us, the practical upshot is simple: a container bound for your project comes in over the neighborhood's own local street access, not staged off the larger Belair road network across the highway. We know the approach, and we plan the drop around it.

Once we are in, the streets tell us where to go. Heather Hills follows Levitt's Belair convention to the letter — every street name in the section begins with H, and that first letter is the main way to tell which section a home sits in. It is the first thing our drivers confirm when they turn in, which cuts down on wrong-address runs that slow a delivery. The quarter-acre lots give you real private driveways, and for a one-room reno that is exactly what you want: the 15-yard stages on your own driveway rather than tying up a narrow H-street. A 15-yard needs about 16 feet of straight length plus a little clearance and an open swing for the rear door, and most Heather Hills driveways take that comfortably. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface, and because the 15 is shorter and lower than the bigger containers, it is the size that keeps your project on your own property.

**The Bowie permit rule, and why it rarely bites here.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. Because these quarter-acre Levitt lots came with their own private driveways, a street permit is the exception in Heather Hills rather than the rule — most 15-yard drops here never touch the right-of-way at all. If the only practical spot turns out to be the street, that permit goes through the city, and we will flag it the moment we look at your address rather than letting you find out on delivery day. There is a Heather Hills homeowners association presence in the neighborhood, so if your project falls under any placement rules, give them a heads-up and we can provide a service confirmation to forward along.
