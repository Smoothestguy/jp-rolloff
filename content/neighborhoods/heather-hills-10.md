## Getting the can in — and the Bowie permit rule

Here's where Heather Hills's layout actually matters for delivery. Because the section is the only Belair piece south of US 50 with no road link to the rest of the community, every dumpster for a Heather Hills project has to be hauled in over the neighborhood's own local street access — we can't stage off the larger Belair road network and roll over. Our trucks come straight in to your H-street address. That's no trouble for us; it's just the reality of a section that was given its own elementary school and a footbridge instead of a through-road. Knowing it ahead of time is part of getting in and out cleanly.

And on these quarter-acre lots, placement realistically happens on the home's own driveway rather than tying up a narrow residential street. That works in your favor on the permit question. The City of Bowie only requires a right-of-way permit when a dumpster sits in a city street — driveways and HOA-approved staging do not trigger it. So most 10-yard drops here, sitting on your own driveway, need no permit at all. There's a Heather Hills Homeowners Association presence in the neighborhood, so if your staging plan touches any shared or association-controlled space, a quick approval there keeps everything tidy. If the only workable spot truly is the street, just say so when you call and we'll walk you through the right-of-way permit so you're covered. Because the 10-yard's footprint is so small, clearing a single-car spot on the driveway the night before is usually all the prep you'll need.
