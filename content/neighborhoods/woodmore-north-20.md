## What fits in a 20-yard dumpster

The 20-yard measures 16 ft long by 8 ft wide by 5 ft tall and holds roughly six pickup-truck loads of debris. Pricing here is per material, and a 20-yard **starts at $620 for construction debris (other materials priced separately; aggregates are accepted only in the 10, 15, and 20-yard)**. That price includes delivery, pickup on request, and a 14-day rental with a 2-ton (4,000 lb) weight allowance — extra days run $5/day, and any weight over the 2-ton allowance is billed at $105 per additional ton.

Those five feet of wall height are the part people underestimate. The wall is low enough that you can still walk debris up and toss it over the side, but tall enough to swallow the volume of a genuine project. That balance is why the 20-yard sits in the sweet spot: bigger than a quick-cleanup 10 or 15-yard, but short of the 25 or 30-yard containers that mostly make sense when you take a whole house to the studs. It is the size most customers end up choosing — and rarely regret. It even has a nickname for the role it plays best: the "whole-house cleanout" size.

## Why the 20-yard fits Woodmore North specifically

The homes here are large and they are now old enough — twenty-plus years on the earliest of them — to be hitting their first big round of updates. That changes the kind of work a homeowner takes on. Where Bowie's older, tightly-platted Levitt-era neighborhoods, with their small lots and slab-built ranchers, tend toward modest interior jobs, Woodmore North homes have multiple finished levels, finished basements, and four to six baths apiece. A project here is measured in rooms, not in a single bathroom — and that is squarely 20-yard territory.

Here are the jobs that come up again and again across Woodmore North:

- **Whole-house cleanouts.** This is the 20-yard's best-known role. Clearing out one of these 5,000-to-8,400-square-foot homes between owners, or before a top-to-bottom refresh, fills a 20-yard about right without paying for space you will not use.
- **Multi-room remodels.** A kitchen-and-baths update, or a run of rooms getting new flooring and drywall, is the classic 20-yard job in a 2000s home reaching the age where builder-grade finishes start to date. With four-to-six-bath floor plans here, a multi-room refresh throws off real volume.
- **Estate cleanouts.** When a longtime Woodmore North home finally changes hands, the 20-yard handles furniture, boxes, and household debris in a single container, finished basement included.
- **Mid-size roofing tear-offs.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off on one of these multi-story homes fits comfortably; the 14-day rental covers a typical Woodmore North roofline, and any weight beyond the 2-ton allowance is billed at $105 per additional ton.

If your project is smaller — a single bathroom, a garage purge, a few rooms of flooring — a 15-yard may serve you better. ## Placement, permits, and the HOA in Woodmore North

**Driveway placement is the rule here.** This is the detail that sets Woodmore North apart from older Bowie neighborhoods. The lots are wide — commonly around 0.73 acre, with some parcels reaching toward an acre or more — and the homes sit back behind long private driveways. That means a 20-yard almost always stages on your own driveway with room to spare rather than out in the street. The container needs roughly 16 feet of straight length plus a few feet of clearance and an open swing for the rear barn doors, and a Woodmore North driveway typically gives us all of that and then some. We will lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**The City of Bowie permit, and when it actually applies.** Bowie only requires a right-of-way permit if the dumpster sits in a city street — driveways and HOA-approved staging do not need one. Because nearly every Woodmore North project stages on a private driveway, that street permit is the exception here, not the rule. If your only practical spot turns out to be the street, the permit goes through Bowie's Public Works Department, and we will flag it the moment we look at your address. For most homes on Woodmore North Boulevard and the surrounding streets, it simply will not come up.

**The HOA is the box to check.** With street permitting off the table for most homes, the gatekeeper that actually matters here is the homeowners' association. The Stewart's Landing HOA serving this area is administered through Quiza Management, and an HOA approval step for dumpster placement and staging is the local consideration to plan around. A quick call to your association before your delivery date is worth it — confirm where they are comfortable with the container sitting and for how long, and the drop goes smoothly. If your community has its own rules about screening or placement on the driveway apron, tell us up front and we will plan the drop accordingly.

**Drive time and getting around.** We are about 25 minutes out in Beltsville and dispatch every morning by Route 197 or the BW Parkway, so we know exactly where these streets are. Woodmore North is reached via Annapolis Road (MD-450) and Hillmeade Road, in the larger upscale Woodmore section of northern Bowie. Most addresses here are scheduled inside 24 hours, with an early call often making the same-day run.
