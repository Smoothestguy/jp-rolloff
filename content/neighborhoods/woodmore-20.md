## What fits in a 20-yard dumpster

The 20-yard measures 22 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall and holds roughly nine pickup-truck loads of debris. Pricing is per material — for a 20-yard it **includes delivery, pickup on request, and a 14-day rental with a 2-ton (4,000 lb) weight allowance, priced by material, so call for a quote**, and aggregates are accepted only in the 10, 15, and 20-yard sizes. Keep the box longer for $5 a day, and any weight over the 2-ton allowance is billed at $105 per additional ton. Pickup is on request, so just call to schedule it when you are ready.

Those four feet of wall height are part of what makes the 20-yard so easy to load. The wall is low enough that you can still walk debris up and toss it over the side, while the box's 22-foot length swallows the volume of a real project. That balance is why the 20-yard sits in the sweet spot for so many Woodmore homes: bigger than a quick-cleanup 10 or 15-yard, but not the oversized 30-yard that mostly makes sense for new construction or full structural teardown. It is the size most customers end up choosing — and rarely regret.

## Why the 20-yard fits Woodmore specifically

Woodmore is large-lot luxury stock. Homes here sit on generous lots — Woodmore Highlands runs to roughly one-acre parcels with 5,000-plus-square-foot brick and Colonial Revival homes — and broader home values land somewhere in the $550,000 to $1.3 million range. That price tier drives a particular kind of work: not small touch-ups, but substantial renovations, multi-room remodels, and estate transitions in homes that have real square footage to clear. The 20-yard is built for exactly that scale of single-house project, measured in rooms rather than excavated dirt.

Here are the jobs that come up again and again across Woodmore:

- **Whole-house cleanouts.** This is the 20-yard's best-known role — it is even nicknamed the "whole-house cleanout" size. Clearing a 5,000-square-foot Woodmore home between owners, or before a top-to-bottom refresh, fills a 20-yard about right without paying for space you will not use.
- **Multi-room remodels.** A kitchen-and-baths gut, or a run of rooms getting new flooring and millwork, is the classic 20-yard job in homes at this tier where owners update finishes to match the value of the address. Pulling cabinetry, tile, and a bath or two generates exactly the debris volume a 20 was built for.
- **Estate cleanouts.** When a longtime Woodmore home finally changes hands, the 20-yard handles furniture, boxes, and household debris in a single container — a common project in an established, affluent community where homes pass between generations and owners.
- **Mid-size roofing tear-offs.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off on one of these large Colonial Revival rooflines fits comfortably, with the 2-ton allowance covering most jobs of that size.

If your project is smaller — a single bathroom, a garage purge, a few rooms of flooring — a 15-yard may serve you better. If you are taking the house to the studs or adding on, we will talk through whether a 30 makes more sense. Tell us the address and the scope and we will steer you straight; we would rather size it right than oversell you.

## Placement, permits, and the gate in Woodmore

**Gate access is the real logistics issue here.** Pleasant Prospect Road is legally a public county road, but the homeowners association has installed electronic gates on both ends and runs a gatehouse at the western entrance. Residents use key cards; non-residents — including contractors and delivery trucks — either need a gate card or have to stop at the gatehouse and state their business to the Association agent on duty. In practice that means our truck cannot simply roll up to your home unannounced. We arrange access before delivery: you let the gatehouse and your HOA know JP Roll-Off is coming on a given morning, or you plan to meet the truck at the gate and escort it in. Sort that out in advance and the drop goes smoothly; skip it and a truck sits idling at a guardhouse. We will walk through the access plan with you when you book.

**Driveway placement on one-acre lots.** The good news is that once the truck is through the gate, placement is rarely a problem. Woodmore's wide driveways and large lots almost always let a 20-yard stage on-site rather than in the street. The container needs roughly 22 feet of straight length plus a few feet of clearance and an open swing for the rear door, and these estate driveways generally have room to spare. We will lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**Golf-course frontage.** Many Woodmore lots back onto or sit adjacent to the Arnold Palmer-designed championship course inside the community. If your home is one of them, we keep the container off course frontage and clear of cart paths — tell us where the property line meets the course and we will place accordingly.

**Confirm which HOA governs your address.** Woodmore spans several sub-developments built across different eras — Woodmore North, Woodmore Estates, Woodmore Highlands, Woodmore Meadows, and Woodmore South — and each carries its own HOA with its own staging and placement rules. It is worth a quick call to your association before your delivery date to confirm where a container can sit and whether any approval is needed.

**The city permit is usually a non-issue.** The City of Bowie only requires a right-of-way permit if the dumpster sits in a city street — driveways and HOA-approved staging do not need one. Because nearly every Woodmore project stages on a private driveway, that street permit is the exception here, not the rule. If your only practical spot does turn out to be the road, that permit goes through Bowie's Public Works Department, and we will flag it the moment we look at your address.

**Drive time and getting around.** We are about 25 minutes out in Beltsville and dispatch every morning by Route 197 or the BW Parkway. Woodmore sits in Bowie/Mitchellville in Prince George's County, with Woodmore Road running east-west through its center. Once gate access is arranged, most addresses here are scheduled within 24 hours, and an early call may still make a same-day run.
