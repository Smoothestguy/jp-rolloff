## Why the 15-yard fits a Woodmore North home

The 15-yard measures 14 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall and holds about four and a half pickup-truck loads of debris. It carries a 1.5-ton (3,000 lb) weight allowance, and a typical Woodmore North rental runs **$425 to $485, including delivery, pickup, and that tonnage** — no tipping-fee surprises tacked on after the haul.

We call this the "single-room reno" size, and it is the in-between container — bigger than a 10-yard quick cleanup, but still short and low enough to stay driveway-friendly. In a neighborhood like Woodmore North, that driveway question is almost always an easy one. With lots around three-quarters of an acre and frequent long private driveways, a 15-yard stages on your own pad with room to spare — no need to put it anywhere near a city street. The compact 14-foot footprint also means it leaves plenty of the driveway open for contractor trucks and daily comings and goings, which matters on a home where the renovation may run for weeks.

So the 15 fits on two counts: it swallows a contained, one-room project inside one of these large homes, and it stages cleanly on the kind of driveway Woodmore North homes have. If you are taking on a whole-house gut or clearing an entire estate at once, you may want to step up to a 25 or 30, which these big lots have no trouble accommodating. But for a single defined project, the 15 is the right-sized tool — you are not paying for air on a container half-full.

Because Woodmore North homes are large and now twenty-plus years into their lifespan, the work here tends to be the first serious round of updates on homes that were high-spec to begin with. A 15-yard is built for exactly that: the larger debris of one part of a big house at a time.

## Placement, HOA approval, and the Bowie permit rule

**In Woodmore North, the HOA is the gatekeeper — not a city permit.** This is the detail that sets the neighborhood apart from Bowie's older, tightly-platted Levitt-era tracts with their small lots and narrow streets. Woodmore North sits under the Stewart's Landing HOA, administered at 13605 Ulysses Court, Bowie, MD 20720; an HOA exists here, so dumpster placement and staging should follow its approval. For most drops, the relevant sign-off is HOA architectural and staging approval, not a city street permit — and because the lots are so generous, the approved spot is almost always your own driveway.

**The Bowie permit rule.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. In Woodmore North, with long private driveways on nearly every parcel, the container almost never needs to touch the street, so the right-of-way permit rarely comes into play. In the uncommon case that your only workable spot truly puts the dumpster in a city street, we will flag the permit the moment we look at your address so there are no surprises.

**Lots and driveways.** Woodmore North's roughly three-quarter-acre lots give a 15-yard plenty of staging room, and the long driveways mean you can place it well off the street and still leave space for everything else.
