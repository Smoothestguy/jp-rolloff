## Driveway placement keeps you clear of Bowie's permit rule

Here's the practical detail that makes Meadowbrook straightforward. The City of Bowie only requires a right-of-way permit when a dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging don't trigger it. Because Levitt built every one of these homes with its own paved driveway, the overwhelming majority of our Meadowbrook deliveries stage on private property and never touch the pavement out front — which means no permit, no city paperwork, and no delay.

If your particular situation does require the container to sit in the street — an unusual driveway angle, a repaving project, something out of the ordinary — the office to contact is the City of Bowie's Public Works Engineering Review & Permitting division, which handles obstructions in the public right-of-way. We're happy to talk through it with you before delivery so there are no surprises. In our experience, though, a 20-yard on a standard Meadowbrook driveway almost never needs any of that.

We'll also walk the placement with you. A 22-foot container needs a clear, reasonably level run of driveway and enough overhead room for the truck to lift and set it down. On these lots that's rarely a problem, but it's worth a 30-second look so we drop it exactly where it's easiest for you to load.
