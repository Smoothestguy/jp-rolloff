## What fits in a 10-yard dumpster

The 10-yard is the smallest footprint we run, measuring 12 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall. It holds roughly three pickup-truck loads of debris and comes with a 1-ton (2,000 lb) weight allowance. In Somerset a 10-yard **includes delivery, pickup on request, and a 14-day rental with that 1-ton allowance — priced by material, so call us for a quote**. Go over the allowance and additional tonnage is billed at $105 per ton; extra days run $5 a day.

Those low four-foot walls are the quiet advantage on an older Levitt home. You can walk right up and drop debris over the side without a ramp or a heave, which matters when you are loading the heavy, dense material these houses give up — old tile, plaster, dirt, roofing scrap. The 1-ton allowance is sized for exactly that kind of focused job: a single-room remodel or a one-corner cleanout, not a whole-house demolition. And because the container fits inside a single car spot, it slides onto a standard Somerset driveway without swallowing the whole thing. If your project outgrows one room, we will steer you to a 15 or 20-yard instead — but for the jobs below, the 10 is the right call.

## Which Somerset jobs suit the 10-yard

This is the size folks know as the "garage cleanout," and on these original Levitt lots that is the call we field most. The four jobs that land squarely in a 10-yard around Somerset:

- **Garage cleanouts.** The garages and carports on these 1960s Cape Cods and ranchers fill a 10-yard about right — years of paint cans, warped lumber, broken tools, and boxes come up fast. It is the classic single-bay clear-out without paying for space you will not use.
- **Bathroom remodels.** A single-bath gut in an original Somerset home — old tile, the tub, vanity, drywall, and subfloor — is the textbook 10-yard job. The dense tile and plaster stay well inside the 1-ton allowance for one bathroom in a house this size.
- **Yard waste and trees.** Sixty-five years of growth means mature trees and overgrown beds on a lot of these S-street lots. Brush, branches, sod, and a removed tree or two load neatly into the low-walled 10, and the 1-ton allowance is sized for a single yard's worth of green, heavy debris.
- **Estate downsize.** When a long-held family home in Somerset changes hands — and on the oldest tract in Bowie, plenty do — a 10-yard handles the furniture, household goods, and accumulated clutter of a measured downsize. It is the right size for emptying a home a room at a time rather than a full gut.

If you are taking on a kitchen tear-out, a roof, siding, or a whole interior gut at once, those run heavier and bulkier than a 10 is built for, and we will tell you so up front rather than watch you overfill it. The 10-yard earns its keep on the one-room, one-corner, one-weekend jobs that these original Levitt homes throw off.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Driveway placement is the default in Somerset.** These homes were laid out with standard single-width driveways, and the 10-yard was built for exactly that — its single-car-spot footprint tucks onto a Somerset drive without blocking the second car or the walk. A 10-yard needs about 12 feet of straight length plus a little clearance and a clear swing for the rear door. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface, which is worth doing on the older driveways out here.

**Finding the door is easy on the S-streets.** Belair at Bowie was platted with an alliterative quirk that still helps our drivers: every street in a section starts with the same letter as the section name, so in Somerset they all begin with "S" — Sussex Lane and the rest. Give us the address and the truck knows it is hunting an S-street, which keeps the morning drop quick and predictable.

**The City of Bowie permit rule.** Somerset sits inside the City of Bowie, and the city only requires a right-of-way permit when a dumpster has to sit in a city street. A container placed entirely on your private driveway or on HOA-approved staging does **not** require the permit. With driveway placement the norm on these lots, most Somerset jobs never trigger it — and if your address is the rare case that does, we will flag it before we roll.

**Drive time.** Somerset is about 25 minutes from our Beltsville yard, an easy run up Route 197 or the BW Parkway. We dispatch every morning, so most Somerset addresses are scheduled within 24 hours, and an early call may still make a same-day drop.
