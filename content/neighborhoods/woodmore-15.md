## Why the 15-yard fits a Woodmore home

The 15-yard measures 14 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall and holds about four and a half pickup-truck loads of debris. It carries a 1.5-ton (3,000 lb) weight allowance, and a Woodmore rental **starts at $595 for construction debris (other materials priced separately), including delivery, a 14-day rental, and that 1.5-ton allowance** — pickup is on request, so call to schedule. Pricing is per material rather than flat, and any weight beyond the 1.5-ton allowance is $105 per additional ton.

We call this the "single-room reno" size, and it is the in-between container — bigger than a 10-yard quick cleanup, but still short and low enough to stay driveway-friendly for most homes. On Woodmore's one-acre estate lots, driveway room is rarely the constraint; these are wide, deep drives that can easily stage a 15 on your own property rather than the street. The reason we still reach for the 15 rather than a larger box on these big lots is the job, not the lot. For a single, contained project — one kitchen, one roofline, a pair of baths, a basement — the 15 swallows the debris without you paying for empty space, and its short 14-foot footprint also keeps the truck's footprint modest once it is through the gate and onto your property.

If you are gutting an entire estate at once or clearing a full turnover, you may want to step up to a 20 or larger. For one defined room or system at a time, the 15 is the size we drop most often in Woodmore.

## Which Woodmore projects suit the 15-yard

Woodmore is one of the most affluent communities in the county, and at this price tier the work tends to be renovation and estate-refresh projects rather than new construction. That is exactly what a 15-yard is built for — larger debris from one part of the house at a time.

- **Kitchen remodel.** Tearing out cabinets, counters, and flooring from a Woodmore kitchen — drywall included — fills a 15-yard about right, without paying for space you will not use.
- **Roofing tear-off.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off over one of these large brick Colonials sits comfortably inside the 1.5-ton allowance, so the load weight stays in line with the price.

## Gate access is the real Woodmore logistics question

Here is what sets Woodmore apart from nearly every other Bowie neighborhood: the flagship community is behind a guardhouse, and the only road through it — Pleasant Prospect Road, the horseshoe-shaped road branching off Woodmore Road — is gated on both ends. Residents use key cards. Non-residents, contractors and delivery trucks included, generally cannot just roll up; access is controlled by the gatehouse on the western end, where a driver typically needs pre-clearance or a resident escort to reach the home.

What that means in practice: **the single most important thing you can do before we deliver is arrange guardhouse access.** Let your HOA or the Association agent at the gate know that JP Roll-Off is coming, on what day, and roughly when. If your section requires a gate card or a name on a cleared-vendor list for our truck, get that set up ahead of the drop. When you call us, tell us how your gate works — whether we check in at the guardhouse and state our purpose, whether you will meet the truck, or whether there is a vendor pass to pick up. We handle these gated deliveries regularly and would rather sort the access out on the phone than have a loaded truck idling at a closed gate.

It is worth knowing that Woodmore is not a single HOA, either. The community spans distinct sub-developments — Woodmore North, Woodmore Estates, Woodmore Highlands, Woodmore Meadows, Woodmore South — each with its own association and its own staging and placement rules. So part of pre-clearing a drop is simply confirming which HOA governs your specific address and what it asks for. Tell us your section and we will work to its rules.

## Placement, the golf course, and the Bowie permit rule

**On a Woodmore lot, the driveway almost always wins.** Those one-acre estate lots and wide driveways mean a 15-yard can nearly always stage on your own property rather than in the street — which sidesteps the city permit entirely. The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if a dumpster sits in a city street; driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. So in Woodmore the practical placement conversation is about your driveway and your HOA's rules, not a city street permit. In the rare case the only workable spot truly puts the container in a public street, we will flag the right-of-way permit the moment we look at your address.

**Mind the course frontage.** Many Woodmore lots back onto or sit adjacent to the Arnold Palmer course and its 42 acres of lakes and cart paths. If your home is on a golf-adjacent lot, we will place the container to keep it off course frontage and clear of cart paths — tell us which side of the property faces the course and we will square the drop accordingly.

**Protecting the drive.** These are high-end driveways, and we treat them that way. Give us the spot and any constraints — a gate, a turn, a slope, course frontage — and we will position the 15 to fit and lay boards under the wheels on request.
