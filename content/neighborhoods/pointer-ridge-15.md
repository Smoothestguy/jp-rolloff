## Why the 15-yard fits a Pointer Ridge home

The 15-yard measures 16 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall and holds about seven pickup-truck loads of debris. Pricing is per material — the 15-yard **includes delivery, pickup, and a 14-day rental with a 1.5-ton (3,000 lb) weight allowance, priced by what you are throwing out, so call for a quote**. Extra weight beyond the allowance is $105 per additional ton, and the extra days run $5/day if you keep it past 14. Pickup is on request — just call to schedule the haul.

We call this the "single-room reno" size, and it earns that name on these P-streets. The 15 is the in-between container — bigger than a 10-yard quick cleanup, but still short and low enough to stay driveway-friendly. That matters less for clearance here than it does in Bowie's tighter townhouse enclaves, because Pointer Ridge homes sit on roughly third-of-an-acre lots with private driveways and room to spare. What the 15 really does on a lot this size is keep you from overpaying: it is sized to one defined project rather than a whole-house overhaul, so you are not renting empty air in a 20- or 30-yard box for a job that fills half of it.

So the 15 fits on the scope, not the squeeze. If you are gutting an entire 1970s rambler down to the studs or clearing a full estate turnover, step up to a 20. For one room, one roof, or one cleanout in a Pointer Ridge home, the 15 is the one we drop most often.

## Which Pointer Ridge projects suit the 15-yard

Because the housing stock here is original 1970s and '80s construction — ranches, ramblers, and Colonials now four and five decades old — the work that comes up is aging-home modernization, not new-build debris. These are the homeowners hitting the renovation years all at once, and the 15-yard is sized right for the projects that take on one part of the house at a time:

- **Kitchen remodel.** Tearing out the original cabinets, counters, flooring, and a wall of dated drywall from a Pointer Ridge kitchen fills a 15-yard about right, without paying for space you will not use.
- **Roofing tear-off.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off over a rambler or Colonial roof span sits comfortably inside the 1.5-ton allowance — heavy enough to need the weight room, contained enough to stay in one box.
- **Two-bath gut.** Pulling tile, vanities, tubs, and subfloor from a pair of bathrooms in a 40-to-50-year-old home is a classic 15-yard job, common as these houses move into their second or third round of updates.
- **Basement cleanout.** Clearing decades of accumulation, old paneling, and carpet from a lower level lands neatly in a 15 — a frequent need as long-held Pointer Ridge homes change hands or get refreshed.

Because so many of these homes came out of the same Levitt-era expansion of Belair at Bowie, we have a good sense of what one room's worth of demo weighs before we ever pull up. If you are not sure whether your project is a 15 or a 20, tell us the room and the scope. We have hauled enough of these remodels to point you the right way before you commit.

## Placement, permits, and the P-street layout

**Most Pointer Ridge projects stage right on the driveway.** This is the real advantage out here, and it is what sets the neighborhood apart from Bowie's HOA-governed, shared-wall tracts. Generous third-of-an-acre detached lots with grassy front lawns, stone walkways, and private driveways give most single-family projects room to place the roll-off on-property rather than in the street. The 15-yard's 16-foot footprint tucks onto a typical Pointer Ridge driveway with room to work around it, and staging on your own property keeps the whole job simpler.

**The Bowie permit rule.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. Since most Pointer Ridge homes can stage the 15 on their own driveway, the city permit rarely comes up at all. If your only workable placement truly puts the container in a city street, we will flag the right-of-way permit the moment we look at your address so there are no surprises.

**The HOA question is the exception, not the rule.** Many Pointer Ridge single-family listings explicitly note no HOA, which means most detached-home projects have no association approval step for a temporary dumpster. The exception is the minority of attached and townhome clusters in the section — Pointer Ridge Place, for instance, shows attached and multi-unit addresses alongside the detached homes. If you are in one of those clusters, it is worth confirming whether your group is governed before we stage; the City of Bowie keeps a homeowners-and-civic-association list that can help you check. For the typical detached P-street home, there is no such step.

**Keeping the school route clear.** Pointer Ridge Elementary sits inside the neighborhood at 1110 Parkington Lane, serving K-5 and within walking distance of many homes. If your address is near the school, we will set the container to keep the sidewalk and the walking route clear — a small thing that matters when kids are coming and going.

**Finding your door.** The all-"P" street naming makes Pointer Ridge instantly readable to a local dispatcher and driver, so a clear address and cross-street get our truck to the right block fast. We are about 25 minutes out in Beltsville and dispatch every morning by Route 197 or the BW Parkway. Most Pointer Ridge addresses are scheduled within 24 hours, and an early call may still make a same-day run.
