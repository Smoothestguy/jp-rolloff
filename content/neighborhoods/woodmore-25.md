## What fits in a 25-yard dumpster

The 25-yard measures 18 ft long by 8 ft wide by 6 ft tall and holds roughly ten-and-a-half pickup-truck loads of debris. Pricing is per material — construction debris in the 25-yard is **priced by material, so call for a quote**, with other materials priced separately. Every rental includes delivery, pickup on request, and a **14-day rental with a 2-ton (4,000 lb) weight allowance**; additional days are $5/day. The 2 tons are included, but excess weight is billed at $105 per additional ton, so we will talk through the likely weight before we drop the container.

The thing to understand about the 25 is that it is genuinely large. With 6-foot walls it is the size where you need to measure twice before booking, because it asks for real room: a clear, straight run of driveway with open space at the back for the rear door to swing. In return you get the volume to keep a major job moving without calling for a swap halfway through. That is why it is the size contractors reach for on a build that runs for weeks rather than a weekend.

## The gate is the real logistics here — not the permit

Most Bowie neighborhoods let a hauler simply back into a driveway and drop the box. Woodmore is different, and it is worth being plain about why. The flagship gated community — planned around 398 single-family homes on 799 acres, centered on the Country Club at Woodmore — is reached by a single road, **Pleasant Prospect Road**, a horseshoe-shaped county road that branches off Woodmore Road. Access is controlled by a gatehouse on the western end. Residents use key cards; non-residents, including contractors and delivery trucks, have to either carry a gate card or stop at the gatehouse and state their purpose to an Association agent.

What that means in practice is simple: **a roll-off truck cannot just show up.** A 25-yard delivery needs to be pre-cleared. The smoothest jobs are the ones where you, the resident, either meet our driver at the gate, leave your name with the gatehouse ahead of delivery morning, or arrange a gate card so the truck rolls straight through. We will coordinate a delivery window with you so you know roughly when to expect us and can have the guardhouse expecting our truck by name. Get that one detail right and a Woodmore drop is no harder than any other; skip it and a 20-foot truck sits idling outside a gate it cannot open.

## Why the street permit almost never comes up

The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only when a dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. In Woodmore this rule rarely bites, and the reason is the lots: at roughly one acre with wide, long driveways, these estate homes can almost always stage a 25-yard entirely on your own property. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface, and on a drive this size there is generally a spot well clear of the garage and the turnaround. So the defining issue here is not the permit — it is the gate. If your particular block does happen to require a street placement, we will flag it the moment we look at your address rather than letting you find out on delivery morning.
