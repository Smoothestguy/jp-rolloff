## What fits in a 20-yard dumpster

The 20-yard measures 16 ft long by 8 ft wide by 5 ft tall and holds roughly six pickup-truck loads of debris. It comes with a 2-ton (4,000 lb) weight allowance, and a typical Amber Meadows rental runs **$450 to $525, including delivery, pickup, and that tonnage** — no tipping-fee surprise tacked on after the haul.

Those five feet of wall height are the part people tend to underestimate. The wall is low enough to walk debris up and pitch it over the side, but tall enough to swallow the volume of a genuine project. That balance is why the 20-yard sits in the sweet spot for so many homes here: bigger than a quick-cleanup 10 or 15-yard, but not the oversized 30-yard that mostly makes sense for new construction or full structural demolition. It is the size most customers end up choosing — and rarely regret.

## Placement, permits, and staging in Amber Meadows

**The lots and driveways here are a real advantage.** Amber Meadows homes sit on quarter-to-half-acre lots along sidewalk-lined streets, with attached garages and private driveways and the kind of generous setbacks you do not get in Bowie's denser townhome tracts. A 20-yard needs about 16 feet of straight length plus a few feet of clearance and an open swing for the rear door, and most of these driveways take that with room to spare. That means the container sits on your own property rather than spilling into the lane out front. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**The Bowie permit rule, and why it rarely bites here.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. Because almost every project in Amber Meadows can stage on the owner's own driveway or lawn, a city street permit is the exception rather than the rule — most 20-yard drops here never touch the right-of-way at all. If the only practical spot turns out to be the street, that permit goes through the city, and we will flag it the moment we look at your address rather than letting you find out on delivery day.
