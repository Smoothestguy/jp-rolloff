## What fits in a 25-yard dumpster

The 25-yard is our major-construction size. It measures 20 ft long by 8 ft wide by 5 ft tall and holds roughly 7.5 pickup-truck loads of debris. Pricing is per material: in Jericho Park a 25-yard for construction debris **starts at $640 (other materials priced separately)**, and that price includes delivery, pickup on request, and a 14-day rental with a 2-ton weight allowance. Need it longer? It's just $5 per extra day, and any weight over the 2-ton allowance is billed at $105 per additional ton.

The jump from a 20-yard is real volume, not just a couple of extra feet of steel. That capacity is what keeps a single container on site through a project that would otherwise force a second haul partway through — a whole-house remodel, a substantial addition, a large roof, or a full cleanout. The walls are still five feet, so a crew can walk debris up and toss it over the side without a ramp, but there is meaningfully more room behind those walls than the mid-size containers offer.

One piece of advice we give every customer on this size: **measure twice before booking.** Twenty feet of straight container, plus clearance for the truck and the rear swing door, is a long footprint. The good news in Jericho Park is that the lots were built for it — the long private driveways here swallow that length easily — but it is still worth confirming the spot before delivery day rather than after.

## Which Jericho Park jobs suit the 25-yard

The housing stock is the story here. Jericho Park runs to midcentury single-family homes alongside contemporary farmhouse-style new builds, with custom-built houses and multi-acre parcels in the mix. Listings in the area have included half-acre, 1.18-acre, 1.25-acre, and 1.5-acre lots — even a rare 8-acre parcel within walking distance of Bowie State University. Older midcentury homes age into heavy renovation work, the custom and new builds drive additions and ground-up debris, and the big parcels generate land and lot cleanouts. Those are the jobs the 25-yard is built for:

- **Home additions.** Bumping out a midcentury rancher or adding onto a custom home throws off framing lumber, old siding, and tear-out debris in the volume a 25-yard is meant to hold — more than a smaller box can take without a mid-project swap.
- **Whole-house remodels.** A full gut of an older Jericho Park home — kitchen, baths, flooring, and drywall all coming out at once — is a textbook 25-yard job. When a midcentury house gets modernized down to the studs, the debris adds up fast, and one large container beats two trips.
- **Large roofing tear-offs.** Midcentury roofs reach the end of the line after enough decades, and the larger custom homes carry a lot of roof. Bigger or multi-layer tear-offs run heavy, so plan for the 2-ton allowance — any weight beyond it is billed at $105 per additional ton.
- **Commercial and large cleanouts.** When a bigger property, an estate on a multi-acre lot, or a business clearout near the university throws off material in bulk, the 25-yard handles it in one go.

If your Jericho Park project is on the smaller side — a single-room redo, a bathroom refresh, or a routine garage cleanout — a 20-yard or even a 15-yard is usually the smarter spend, and we will tell you so. The 25-yard pays off when the job is genuinely big.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Driveway and on-property placement, and Jericho Park is built for it.** This is the real advantage of working on Bowie's large-lot edge. Unlike the tightly packed planned subdivisions elsewhere in the city, the half-acre to multi-acre parcels here come with long private driveways and ample yard room. A 20-foot container — and the truck behind it — fits with room to spare. Most drops here stage entirely on-property, which is usually the right call anyway. We will scout the spot with you and lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**The permit rule is in your favor.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only when a dumpster has to sit in a city street. A container placed entirely on your private driveway or yard does **not** need one. Because Jericho Park homes sit on such generous lots, the vast majority of jobs here stage on-property with no permit at all. If your particular address somehow forces the container into the street, we will flag the permit requirement up front rather than letting it surprise you on delivery day.

**A note on access and staging.** Access into the neighborhood comes off the traffic light onto Jericho Park Road, with Bowie State University at 14000 Jericho Park Road as the landmark. The streets here carry Bowie's equestrian heritage — names like Running Horse Place and Race Horse Lane — and many sit close to preserved open space, with the Patuxent Research Refuge and the Fran Uhler Natural Area along the river to the north. If your block has any HOA or staging guidelines, a quick check never hurts, but the long private driveways mean most homeowners can simply book and go.

**Drive time.** From our Beltsville yard, Jericho Park is about a 25-minute run via Route 197 or the BW Parkway. We dispatch every morning, and same-day delivery is common — so when you are running a build on a deadline, the container shows up when the crew needs it.
