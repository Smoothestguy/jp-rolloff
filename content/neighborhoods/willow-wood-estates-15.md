## Why the 15-yard fits a Willow Wood Estates home

The 15-yard measures 14 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall and holds about four and a half pickup-truck loads of debris. It carries a 1.5-ton (3,000 lb) weight allowance, and a typical Willow Wood rental runs **$425 to $485, including delivery, pickup, and that tonnage** — no tipping-fee surprises added after the haul.

We call this the "single-room reno" size, and it is the in-between container — bigger than a 10-yard quick cleanup, but still short and low enough to stay driveway-friendly for most homes. That matters here. Willow Wood is a neighborhood of midsize detached homes on private, owner-occupied lots, and the 14-foot footprint of the 15 tucks onto a standard suburban driveway far more easily than a 20 or a 30 would. Because these homes are mature but modest in size — not oversized new-construction — a single kitchen, a couple of baths, or one roofline rarely generates the volume that would justify the bigger boxes. The 15 lands right in the sweet spot for the kind of one-part-at-a-time projects these houses produce.

So the 15 fits on two counts: it swallows a contained, single project in a Willow Wood home, and its short footprint stages cleanly on a private driveway. If you are gutting a whole house at once, you may want to step up to a 20. For a single defined room or one tear-off, the 15 is the one we drop most often out here.

## Which Willow Wood Estates projects suit the 15-yard

Because the whole subdivision went up in the same late-1970s-to-early-1980s window, the housing stock is hitting its big-ticket replacement years all at once. Original roofs, original kitchens, original baths, and original decks across a uniform-age neighborhood tend to come due on a similar timeline — neighbors often find themselves tackling the same project a season or two apart. That is exactly what a 15-yard is built for: the larger renovation debris from one part of the house at a time.

- **Kitchen remodel.** Tearing out the original cabinets, counters, and flooring from one of these 1970s-80s kitchens — drywall included — fills a 15-yard about right, without paying for space you will not use.
- **Roofing tear-off.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off over one of these midsize rooflines sits comfortably inside the 1.5-ton allowance, so the load weight stays in line with the price. On 40-plus-year-old homes, the first or second roof replacement is one of the most common reasons we roll out here.
- **Two-bath gut.** Pulling tile, vanities, tubs, and subfloor from a pair of original bathrooms is a classic 15-yard job on a home in its first serious refresh.
- **Basement cleanout.** Clearing decades of accumulated storage — old carpet, framing, paneling, and the contents that pile up over 40 years of ownership — lands neatly in a 15.

Because so many of these homes share the same era, scale, and basic layouts, we have a good sense of what one room's worth of demo weighs before we pull up. If you are not sure whether your project is a 15 or a 20, tell us the room and the scope. ## Placement, driveways, and the Bowie permit rule

**In Willow Wood Estates, the driveway is almost always the answer — and that keeps things simple.** This is a neighborhood of owner-occupied detached homes on private lots, which means the most natural place to stage your 15-yard is your own driveway. That is the detail that sets this kind of project apart from street-placed new-construction work, and it is good news for the paperwork.

**The Bowie permit rule.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. Since nearly every Willow Wood job stages on a private driveway, that permit simply is not triggered — there is nothing extra to file. If your only workable placement truly puts the container in a city street rather than your driveway, we will flag the right-of-way permit the moment we look at your address, and that review runs through Bowie's Public Works Department on Annapolis Road. But for the typical homeowner project out here, the driveway placement keeps you clear of it entirely.

**Lots and driveways.** Willow Wood's midsize detached lots usually give a 15-yard plenty of room on the driveway. The 15's short, low footprint helps it sit square on a standard suburban pad without crowding the garage or the walk. Tell us where you want it and any tight spot — a tree, a slope, a narrow apron — and we will angle the container to fit and lay boards under the wheels on request to protect your driveway surface.

**Finding your door and drive time.** Willow Wood Estates sits in the Bowie/Bowie-Mitchellville area of Prince George's County. We are about 25 minutes out in Beltsville and dispatch every morning by Route 197 or the BW Parkway. Most addresses out here are scheduled inside 24 hours, and an early call often makes the same-day run. A clear address and a known cross-street keep the delivery moving.
