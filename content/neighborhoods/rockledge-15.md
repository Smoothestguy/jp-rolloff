## Placement, permits, and the R-streets

**Driveway placement is the easy part here.** Rockledge is the R-section — the alliterative-by-section naming is the defining wayfinding quirk of every Levitt Bowie neighborhood, and it is how residents identify their part of town. In Rockledge, the streets run through the letter R, from Rockledge Drive itself to Reston Lane. What also defines the section is the build standard: these are detached single-family homes, each with its own private driveway. That is the real advantage of staging a dumpster here. Unlike Bowie's newer townhome or gated communities, nearly every Rockledge job drops on the homeowner's own driveway. A 15-yard needs about 16 feet of straight length plus a little clearance and an open swing for the rear door, and most Rockledge driveways take that comfortably. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**The Bowie permit rule, and why it rarely bites in Rockledge.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. Because nearly every R-section home has its own driveway, a street permit is the exception here, not the rule — most 15-yard drops never touch the right-of-way at all. Getting the container onto the driveway rather than the street is usually the right call on these lots, and it is the placement decision that keeps a permit out of the picture entirely. If the only practical spot turns out to be the street, that permit goes through the city, and we will flag it the moment we look at your address rather than letting you find out on delivery day.

**A note on associations.** Like most of the original Levitt Bowie sections, Rockledge appears to be a non-gated, established subdivision without the mandatory HOA gatekeeping you find in Bowie's newer developments — so staging a dumpster on your private driveway typically does not require board approval. If your block does run a formal association with placement rules, give them a heads-up, and we can provide a service confirmation to forward along.

**Finding your door.** The R-section naming helps our drivers — once we are on Rockledge Drive or Reston Lane, the section reads clearly. Rockledge has a settled, recreation-adjacent feel: the 8.5-acre Rockledge Park sits at 4910 Reston Lane with ballfields, tennis and soccer fields, and picnic shelters, the neighborhood's own Rockledge Elementary anchors the section, and the WB&A Railroad Trail and Bowie Golf Club are close by. A clear address plus a known landmark like Rockledge Park keeps a delivery moving on these residential streets.
