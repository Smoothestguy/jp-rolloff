## What fits in a 25-yard dumpster

The 25-yard is our major-construction size. It measures 20 ft long by 8 ft wide by 5 ft tall and holds roughly 7.5 pickup-truck loads of debris. It comes with a 2.5-ton (5,000 lb) weight allowance, and in Saddle Brook a typical 25-yard rental runs **$525 to $625, including delivery, pickup, and that tonnage** — no tipping-fee surprises tacked on after the haul.

The jump from a 20-yard is real volume, not just a couple more feet of steel. That extra capacity is what keeps one container on site through a project that would otherwise force a second haul partway through — a substantial addition, a whole-house remodel, a large roof, or a full-property cleanout. The walls are still five feet, so a crew can walk debris up and toss it over the side without a ramp, but there is meaningfully more room behind those walls than the mid-size containers offer.

One piece of advice we give every customer on this size: **measure twice before booking.** Twenty feet of straight container, plus clearance for the truck and the rear swing door, is a long footprint. The good news in Saddle Brook is that the lots and driveways were built for larger homes, so the room is usually there — but it is still worth confirming the spot before delivery day rather than after.

## Which Saddle Brook jobs suit the 25-yard

The neighborhood's housing is the whole story. Unlike Bowie's older ramblers and split-levels, Saddle Brook is a late-1990s-to-early-2000s subdivision of larger custom and New Traditional colonials — 2,000 to 5,000-plus square feet, brick fronts, full basements. Renovate a house that size, or clear one out top to bottom, and you generate the kind of volume the 25-yard is built for. These are the jobs that land on this container:

- **Home additions.** Bumping out a full-size colonial or building onto one of the bigger Saddlebrook West homes throws off framing lumber, old siding, and tear-out debris in the volume a 25-yard is meant to hold — more than a smaller box can take without a mid-project swap.
- **Whole-house remodels.** A full gut of a 3,000-plus-square-foot colonial — kitchen, baths, flooring, and drywall all coming out at once, with a finished basement often part of the scope — is a textbook 25-yard job. When a late-'90s house gets modernized down to the studs, one large container beats two trips.
- **Large roofing tear-offs.** These are big roofs on big houses. A larger or multi-layer tear-off runs heavy, and the 2.5-ton allowance gives more headroom for shingle weight than the smaller sizes.
- **Commercial cleanouts.** When a larger property or a nearby business clears out material in bulk, the 25-yard handles it in one go.

If your Saddle Brook project is on the smaller side — a single-room redo, a bathroom refresh, a routine garage purge — a 20-yard or even a 15-yard is usually the smarter spend, and we will tell you so. The 25-yard pays off when the job is genuinely big, which in this neighborhood it often is.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and the HOA

**Driveway placement is the norm here.** Saddle Brook's homes were built with the long private driveways that come with larger lots, and that is the right place to stage a container in this neighborhood. Street placement is rarely necessary and rarely the best choice. We will scout the spot with you and lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the driveway surface — worth doing on the newer concrete and paver drives common in these sections.

**The Bowie permit rule works in your favor.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only when a dumpster has to sit in a city street. A container placed entirely on your private driveway does **not** need one. Because almost every Saddle Brook home has a driveway built for a full-size house, the vast majority of jobs here stage on-property with no permit at all. If your particular address forces the container into the street, we will flag the requirement up front rather than letting it surprise you on delivery day.

**Check with the HOA before you book.** This is the one real difference from Bowie's older civic-association sections. Saddle Brook and Saddlebrook West are served by active homeowners associations — Saddlebrook West's manages common amenities including a pool, two tennis courts, and a playground, and homes here carry quarterly association dues. None of that blocks a dumpster on your own driveway, but if your placement would touch a shared drive or common area, or if your HOA has guidelines on container staging, it is worth a quick call to them before scheduling. We are happy to provide a service confirmation you can forward along. Most homeowners can stage on their own driveway and simply book.

**Drive time.** Saddle Brook sits along the Race Track Road corridor, next to the former Bowie Race Track — the old thoroughbred site the City has been clearing for recreational use — with Saddlebrook East Park and its open field and trails right off Race Track Road at 8311. From our Beltsville yard, that is about a 25-minute run via Route 197 or the BW Parkway.
