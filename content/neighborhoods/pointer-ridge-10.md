## What fits in a 10-yard dumpster

The 10-yard is the smallest footprint we offer, measuring 12 ft long by 8 ft wide by 3.5 ft tall. It holds roughly three pickup-truck loads of debris and comes with a 1-ton (2,000 lb) weight allowance. In Pointer Ridge a typical 10-yard rental runs **$350 to $425, including delivery, pickup, and that tonnage** — no surprise tipping fees sprung on you after the load is weighed.

The low 3.5-foot walls are the quiet advantage. You can walk right up and drop debris over the side without a ramp or a heave, which matters when you are loading heavy, dense material like old bathroom tile, plaster, or yard soil. The 1-ton allowance is sized for exactly that kind of work — a single-room remodel or a focused cleanout, not a whole-house gut. And because the container fits in a single car spot, it tucks neatly onto a Pointer Ridge driveway without swallowing the whole thing. If your project turns out bigger than one room, we will point you to a 15- or 20-yard instead — but for the jobs below, the 10 is the right call.

## Which Pointer Ridge jobs suit the 10-yard

This is the size folks call the "garage cleanout," and around Pointer Ridge that is the call we field most. The neighborhood's housing stock — built mostly in the 1970s and 1980s and now hitting the 40- to 50-year mark — is squarely in the age when kitchens, baths, roofs, and basements start getting refreshed one project at a time. Here are the four jobs that land in a 10-yard on these P streets:

- **Garage cleanouts.** The detached ranches and ramblers in Pointer Ridge sit on roughly third-of-an-acre lots with private driveways, and after four or five decades those garages fill up with paint cans, old lumber, broken tools, and boxes. A single-bay clear-out empties into a 10-yard without overpaying for space, and the container parks on the driveway right where you are working.
- **Bathroom remodels.** A single-bath gut in one of these established homes — the dated tile, the tub, the vanity, drywall, and subfloor — is the textbook 10-yard job. That dense tile and plaster stays well inside the 1-ton allowance for one bathroom, and modernizing the original baths in a 1970s-80s Colonial Revival is one of the most common projects on these streets.
- **Yard waste and trees.** Pointer Ridge lots are known for their grassy front lawns, shrubbery, stone walkways, and tall mature trees, and all that greenery eventually means brush, branches, sod, and the occasional removed tree. It all loads neatly into the low-walled 10. Green yard debris is heavy once it is wet, so the 1-ton allowance is sized for a single yard's worth.
- **Estate downsize.** Many of these homes have been held by the same families since they were built, and as they change hands a 10-yard handles the furniture, household goods, and accumulated clutter of a focused downsize. It is a measured cleanout, not a full whole-house clear-out.

If you are gut-renovating a dated kitchen, finishing a basement, tearing off a roof, or doing a full-home modernization all at once, those run heavier and bulkier than a 10 is built for, and we will tell you so up front. The 10-yard earns its keep on the one-room, one-corner, one-weekend jobs.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Driveway placement is the default in Pointer Ridge, and the neighborhood is built for it.** Most homes here are detached single-family houses on lots running around a third of an acre, each with its own private driveway. That generous, established lot size gives almost every project room to stage the roll-off on-property rather than in the street. A 10-yard needs about 12 feet of straight length plus a little clearance and a clear swing for the rear door, and that fits comfortably on a standard Pointer Ridge driveway. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**The City of Bowie permit rule.** Pointer Ridge is inside the City of Bowie, and the city's rule is straightforward: a right-of-way permit is required **only** if the dumpster sits in a city street. A container placed on your private driveway does not require one. Because nearly every detached home here has its own driveway and a no-HOA setup, the vast majority of our drops in Pointer Ridge never touch the right-of-way and never trigger a permit. The one caution is the minority of attached and townhome clusters on the P streets — addresses like the multi-unit listings along Pointer Ridge Place — where an association may have its own staging rules. If you are in one of those clusters, it is worth a quick check with your association before we deliver; for the detached majority, the driveway is yours to use.

One neighborly note: Pointer Ridge Elementary sits right inside the subdivision at 1110 Parkington Lane, and many homes are within walking distance of it. If your project is near the school, we will set the container so it keeps the sidewalks and the kids' walking route clear.

**Drive time.** Pointer Ridge is about 25 minutes from our Beltsville yard, an easy run down Route 197 or the BW Parkway. We dispatch every morning, so same-day delivery is common when you call early, and the rest of the schedule fills inside 24 hours. You are close enough that getting a truck to a P-section address is never the holdup.
