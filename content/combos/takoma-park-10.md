## What fits in a 10-yard

The 10-yard is best known as the "garage cleanout" size, and its low 4-foot walls are the reason. You walk debris in over the side rather than heaving everything up and over a tall lip — a real advantage when you are clearing a basement or an attic in a century-old house. At 12 x 8 x 4 feet, the box sits inside a single car spot, which matters more in Takoma Park than almost anywhere else we serve.

Think of capacity in pickup-truck terms — about three full loads. That covers a single room or a focused project, and the one-ton weight allowance handles the typical mix of household junk, old fixtures, and light renovation debris. Dense material is the thing to watch: plaster, old tile, brick, and wet debris fill that ton quickly, and gut jobs on these older homes throw off a lot of heavy plaster. If your project is mostly dense material, give us a quick call and we will tell you straight whether 10 yards is enough or whether a bigger box saves you a second haul.

Pricing is per material rather than one price for every load. For construction debris, the 10-yard is **priced by material, so call for a quote** (other materials are priced separately, and heavy aggregates like dirt, concrete, and asphalt are only offered in the 10-, 15-, and 20-yard boxes). The rental includes delivery, pickup, and a 14-day rental with a 1-ton weight allowance; additional days run $5/day, and any weight over the allowance is billed at $105 per additional ton. Pickup is on request, so just call us when you are ready and we will schedule it.

## Which Takoma Park jobs suit this size

Takoma Park is one of the Washington area's oldest planned streetcar suburbs, founded in 1883, and the Takoma Park Historic District holds over 600 contributing buildings dating from the 1880s through the 1930s. The practical result for us: roll-off work here is disproportionately renovation, repair, and cleanout on century-old homes rather than new construction. The housing stock is late-Victorian and early-20th-century — Queen Anne and Stick/Shingle Victorians with ornate woodwork and front porches, Colonial Revival Foursquares, and 1920s Craftsman bungalows. Those homes drive a steady stream of small, room-at-a-time projects, which is exactly what the 10-yard is built for.

- **Garage cleanout.** The signature job for this size. One car spot, low walls, three truck-loads of capacity — clear years of accumulation out of a detached garage or basement in a weekend without renting more box than you need.
- **Bathroom remodel.** A single bathroom's worth of old tile, vanity, tub, and fixtures fits comfortably in 10 yards. The older bungalows and Foursquares around Old Takoma see a constant run of these gut-and-refresh jobs, and the 10-yard's short footprint tucks neatly beside the house.
- **Yard waste and trees.** Branches, brush, and storm cleanup drop easily over the low rail. A word of caution first, though: Takoma Park has one of the toughest tree-protection ordinances in Montgomery County and is a certified Tree City USA. You need a city permit to remove or significantly prune trees, with fines up to $1,000 for violations — so confirm with the city before any major cutting, then let the 10-yard handle the debris.
- **Estate downsize.** Clearing a family home or prepping an inherited Victorian for sale is rarely a 30-yard job. The 10-yard handles a measured, room-by-room downsize, and its small footprint keeps a tight driveway usable while the work stretches across several days.

In the Maple Avenue corridor, the picture shifts a little. That pocket is the city's multi-family area — mid-century apartment buildings and condos like the Maple Avenue Apartments and the Takoma Phoenix on Maple Avenue. For a unit turnover or an HOA project there, placement usually relies on a designated lot space rather than a single-family driveway, and the compact 10-yard is the easiest box to fit into shared parking without blocking neighbors.

## Placement, permits, and drive time

Placement is genuinely the hard part in Takoma Park, and it is where the 10-yard earns its keep. Three constraints collide here in a way they don't in most DMV towns. First, the tree canopy: the same protected hardwoods that make these streets beautiful mean low overhanging limbs are a real concern both when we spot the box and when the truck swings in to deliver and pick up. Second, the terrain — Takoma Park sits on the fall line and is notably hilly, with narrow, gridded streets set at odd angles, and even Carroll Avenue, Old Takoma's main street, pinches to two lanes inside the city. Third, the permit regime. On the small historic-district lots common here, where you physically set the can matters more than how big it is, and the 10-yard's single-spot footprint fits where a larger box simply will not.

On permits: the **City of Takoma Park's Public Works Department issues the permit for placing a container in the public right-of-way (the street), and the city states plainly that no permit is required if the dumpster sits in the driveway.** Many blocks ban street placement entirely, so driveway placement is strongly preferred — and the city warns that you may not set the container until you have received written notice that the permit is granted. We work from that driveway-first rule, and the 10-yard is the easiest size to keep on private property. If your only option is the street, we are glad to talk through the current requirements before we deliver, and we always recommend confirming the specifics with Takoma Park Public Works.

A couple more local notes worth planning around. Properties backing onto Sligo Creek Park tend to have softer, sloped placement options, so we will look for firm ground before we set the box. And because Takoma Park straddles the Montgomery and Prince George's County line, a project on the eastern edge can fall under PG County jurisdiction for right-of-way matters outside the city's reach — another reason a quick call ahead of delivery saves headaches.

As for getting to you: we dispatch from Beltsville, so a Takoma Park delivery is a short run. The tight historic streets mean our driver takes care with placement, but a quick trip keeps our scheduling flexible when a cleanout or remodel timeline shifts on you.
