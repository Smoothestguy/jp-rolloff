## What fits in a 10-yard dumpster

The 10-yard holds roughly three pickup-truck loads. Picture three full beds of debris stacked into a low-walled box you can walk right up to and load over the side. ## Local logistics, permits, and drive time

Here is the part Landover folks get tripped up on, and it is worth getting right before the dumpster shows up.

**Most of Landover is unincorporated Prince George's County.** That means if you set the dumpster on your own driveway or private property, you do not need a county permit at all. Placement in the public right-of-way — the street or curb — is a different story: that falls under the Prince George's County Department of Permitting, Inspections and Enforcement (DPIE), which handles temporary-use and site/road permits out of their Largo office. On the larger lots common in the unincorporated subdivisions, driveway placement is usually easy, so most of our customers here never deal with a permit.

**Landover Hills is the exception.** It is a separate incorporated town, platted back in 1940 and incorporated in 1945, with small wartime-era lots and narrow streets. Because it is incorporated, street or right-of-way placement there requires the town's own permit, not the county's — a different process just a few blocks from neighbors who answer to the county instead. And because those WWII-era lots are tight, driveway space can be at a premium, so street placement (and the town permit) comes up more often in Landover Hills than on the bigger unincorporated lots nearby. If you are not sure which set of rules applies to your address, just ask us when you book — we sort it out for you.

For most of the community, including the FedExField corridor near Northwest Stadium, the homes off Route 202 (Landover Road), and the subdivisions toward the Glenarden border, private-property placement keeps things simple. The Route 202 and Ardwick-Ardmore industrial strip tends to generate bigger jobs that outgrow a 10-yard, but for a tradesperson doing a single bathroom or a homeowner clearing a garage in that part of town, this is the box.

As for getting there: Landover is a short hop from our Beltsville yard, an easy run down the Beltway.
