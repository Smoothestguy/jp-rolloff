## Permits, placement, and the downtown-vs-county question

This is where Upper Marlboro is genuinely different from most DMV towns, and where a quick conversation saves headaches. Before anything else, we'll ask one question: are you in the **historic downtown core** (the Town of Upper Marlboro) or out in the surrounding **Prince George's County** portion of 20772/20774?

If you're inside the town, the Town of Upper Marlboro lets a roll-off sit on **private property for up to 15 days without a town permit**. Past 15 days, or if the container has to go in the town's right-of-way — the street or sidewalk — you'll need a town permit. Town Hall handles those at (301) 627-6905. The town also enforces the underlying Prince George's County construction permits, so on a real addition or remodel, plan for both.

If you're out in the county portion, the same general rule applies: right-of-way placement requires a permit, while a drop on your own private property typically does not. The good news for the rural and estate side of 20772 is that the long private driveways and large lots out toward the Rosaryville and equestrian-center areas easily accommodate a 25-yard — or even a 30 — well clear of the street, so most customers there never touch a permit at all.

A few placement notes that matter for this size specifically: the 18-foot box wants a straight, stable approach, so on the older, narrower streets of the historic district we'll talk through exactly where it can sit. On a large county lot, we have far more flexibility. Either way, keep the spot clear of low branches and soft ground.
