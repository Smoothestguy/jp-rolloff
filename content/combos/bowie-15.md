## What fits in a 15-yard dumpster

The 15-yard measures 14 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall and holds roughly four and a half pickup-truck loads of debris. It comes with a 1.5-ton (3,000 lb) weight allowance, and in Bowie a 15-yard **starts at $595 for construction debris (other materials are priced separately)**. That price includes delivery, a 14-day rental, and pickup on request — you call when you're ready and we come get it. The 1.5-ton allowance is included; if you go over, additional tonnage is billed at $105 per ton, and extra rental days are $5 each.

Four feet of wall height is the part people appreciate once the work starts. It is low enough to walk debris up and toss it over the side without a ramp, which matters on a one-room remodel where you are carrying material out by hand all day. The 15-yard is best known as the "single-room reno" size for exactly that reason: bigger than the 10-yard you would grab for a quick declutter, but not the 20- or 30-yard you would only want for a multi-room gut or full structural demo. For a single project that generates real volume but stays contained, it is the right call.

## Which Bowie jobs suit the 15-yard

Bowie, more than any other DMV town, is a Levitt suburb. The core of residential Bowie is Belair at Bowie, roughly 7,500 near-identical mid-century homes the Levitt firm built between about 1960 and 1965 on the old Belair Estate. Those homes were sold as a handful of repeating models — the Cape Cod, the Rancher, the three- and four-bedroom Colonials, and the Country Clubber — and six decades on they are in their second or third round of renovations. That drives a steady, synchronized wave of exactly the projects a 15-yard handles best:

- **Kitchen remodel.** Pulling original cabinets, countertops, flooring, and drywall from a single Belair kitchen fills a 15-yard about right without paying for space you won't use.
- **Roofing tear-off.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off on one of these mid-century roofs fits comfortably, with the 1.5-ton allowance covering most jobs of that size. Heavier or multi-layer tear-offs may need the next size up, and we will tell you straight.
- **Two-bath gut.** Tile, fixtures, vanities, and subfloor from a pair of bathrooms is a textbook 15-yard load.
- **Basement cleanout.** Clearing out a finished or storage basement — in the newer homes that have them — before a remodel or a sale.

Where the container goes depends on the neighborhood. In the alphabetical Belair sections — Somerset, Buckingham, Kenilworth, Foxhill, and Tulip Grove among them — every home was built on a roughly quarter-acre lot with a real driveway and an enclosed garage, so a 15-yard almost always sits on the homeowner's own driveway. The same holds in Mitchellville and Pointer Ridge. In Old Town Bowie, the oldest part of the city around the 1870s rail line, the Victorian-era houses sit on smaller, older lots with narrower streets, so we scout placement with you. And in Fairwood, Bowie's newer planned community of upscale single-family homes and townhomes built from the mid-2000s on, the single-family sites have plenty of room, while the townhome rows have tighter shared driveways and HOA-approved staging to consider.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Driveway placement.** A 15-yard needs roughly 14 feet of straight length plus a few feet of clearance, and an open swing for the rear door. Because nearly every Belair home was built with a driveway and garage, most Bowie jobs put the container right on private property. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**The Bowie permit rule.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit **only if the dumpster sits in a city street**. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. So if your 15-yard goes on the driveway — which in the Belair sections it almost always can — you are clear. If the only practical spot is the public street, you will need a Bowie right-of-way permit, handled through the city's Public Works Department at 16500 Annapolis Road, and we will flag that when we look at your address.

**HOAs.** Bowie has plenty of active homeowners associations, especially in Fairwood and the townhome sections. Most allow dumpster staging with advance notice but want it on approved placement rather than the street. Give your board a heads-up, and we can provide a service confirmation to forward along.

**Drive time.** We are about 25 minutes out from most Bowie addresses, running either Route 197 or the BW Parkway, and we dispatch every morning. Because Bowie is the largest municipality in Prince George's County and spreads across nearly 19 square miles, that drive time can vary a little depending on which end of the city you are on — Old Town in the north versus Fairwood and Pointer Ridge to the east. Early calls usually make the same-day run; most other addresses are scheduled inside 24 hours.
