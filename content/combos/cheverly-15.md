## What fits in a 15-yard dumpster

The 15-yard measures 14 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall and holds roughly four and a half pickup-truck loads of debris. It comes with a 1.5-ton (3,000 lb) weight allowance, and in Cheverly a 15-yard **starts at $595 for construction debris (other materials priced separately)**. That price includes delivery, pickup, and a 14-day rental with the 1.5-ton allowance; additional days run $5/day, and any weight over the allowance is billed $105 per additional ton. Pickup is on request — just call to schedule when you are done.

Four feet of wall height is the part people come to appreciate once the work starts. It is low enough to walk debris up and toss it over the side without a ramp, which matters on a one-room remodel where you are carrying material out by hand all day. The 15-yard is best known as the "single-room reno" size for exactly that reason: bigger than the 10-yard you would grab for a quick declutter, but not the 20- or 30-yard you would only reach for on a multi-room gut or a full estate cleanout. For a single project that generates real volume but stays contained, it is the right call.

## Which Cheverly jobs suit the 15-yard

Cheverly's housing stock is dominated by detached single-family homes built largely between 1921 and the 1960s — starting with 34 developer-built houses from 1921 to 1925, then filling in with Cape Cods, Colonial Revivals, ramblers, and mid-century ranch and split-level homes. That mix puts a lot of the town's renovation work squarely in 15-yard territory: not the whole-house gut, but the single room or the single system being redone in a house that is decades, sometimes a century, old.

- **Kitchen remodel.** Pulling original cabinets, countertops, flooring, and drywall from one kitchen fills a 15-yard about right without paying for space you won't use.
- **Roofing tear-off.** A single-layer asphalt tear-off on one of these older roofs fits comfortably, with the 1.5-ton allowance covering most jobs of that size. Heavier or multi-layer tear-offs may need the next size up, and we will tell you straight.
- **Two-bath gut.** Tile, fixtures, vanities, and subfloor from a pair of bathrooms is a textbook 15-yard load.
- **Basement cleanout.** Clearing a storage or finished basement before a remodel or a sale.

Cheverly also holds Prince George's County's largest documented collection of 1920s mail-order and kit houses — 34 homes from Sears, Roebuck & Co. and the McClure Homes Company — and the Cheverly Historic District is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. If you are restoring one of these century-old houses, the debris is its own animal: plaster and lath, original wood trim, and dated additions that get peeled back. That kind of restoration is precisely what keeps demand steady for a mid-size 15- or 20-yard roll-off here, and the 15 is usually the right pick when the work is confined to one room or one section of the house at a time.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Driveway placement.** A 15-yard needs roughly 14 feet of straight length plus a few feet of clearance, and an open swing for the rear door. Cheverly was deliberately planned by developer Robert Marshall as an "island of green," with homes set on generous, tree-shaded lots along curving streets — so the practical placement concerns here are different from the flat, grid-platted suburbs around the DMV. The town is a designated Tree City USA with a protected, mature oak canopy, which means low overhanging limbs are a real factor when we position the container and bring the truck in. The terrain is gently rolling rather than flat, so some driveways carry a grade, and we level the container on boards when a slope calls for it. Tell us about your trees and your driveway pitch when you call and we will plan the drop accordingly.

**The Cheverly permit rule.** The Town of Cheverly requires a permit **only if the dumpster sits on the street or public right-of-way**. Driveway placement is permit-free. So if your 15-yard goes on the driveway — which on these single-family lots it usually can — you are clear with no paperwork. If the only practical spot is the street, the town's street-placement permit runs a 24 to 48 hour turnaround, and we will flag that early so it does not hold up your start date.

**Access and drive time.** Cheverly is a compact town of just 1.35 square miles, about a mile from the northeastern edge of Washington, D.C., and U.S. Route 50 borders the community for direct truck access. From Beltsville we are roughly a 20-minute run, and we dispatch every morning. Early calls usually make the same-day drop; most other addresses are scheduled inside 24 hours. The one thing we always account for on Cheverly jobs is the approach — winding, narrow streets and that overhead canopy mean we like to know the address ahead of time so we bring the truck in clean on the first pass.
