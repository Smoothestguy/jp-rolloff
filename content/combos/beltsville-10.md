## What fits in a 10-yard dumpster

The 10-yard is the smallest footprint we offer, measuring 12 ft long by 8 ft wide by 4 ft tall. It holds roughly three pickup-truck loads of debris. Pricing is per material: in Beltsville a 10-yard **includes delivery, pickup on request, and a 14-day rental — priced by material, so call for a quote.** Additional days are available on request when you book, and dense, heavy materials such as concrete, dirt, brick and shingles are quoted separately because weight matters. Pickup is on request — you call us to schedule it.

The low 4-foot walls are the quiet advantage. You can walk right up and drop debris over the side without a ramp or a heave, which matters when you are loading heavy, dense material like tile, dirt, or roofing scrap. The 10-yard is set for that kind of work: a single-room remodel or a focused cleanout, not a whole-house demo. And because the container fits in a single car spot, it slides onto even a narrow Beltsville driveway without eating the whole thing. If your project is bigger than one room, we will point you to a 15 or 20-yard instead — but for the jobs below, the 10 is the honest pick.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Driveway placement is the default here, and for good reason.** So many Beltsville homes front busy state through-roads — US 1 (Baltimore Avenue), MD 198 Powder Mill, Edmonston, Calverton Boulevard — that dropping a container at the curb is rarely practical or allowed. The 10-yard is built for this: its single-car-spot footprint tucks onto even the tighter driveways east of the freeways, like the smaller ranchers around Calverton. A 10-yard needs about 12 feet of straight length plus a little clearance and a clear swing for the rear door. We lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface.

**The Beltsville permit rule.** Beltsville is an unincorporated community, so permitting is handled by Prince George's County rather than a city hall. A dumpster sitting entirely on your private driveway, lawn, or yard does **not** require a permit. A permit is only needed when the container has to occupy the public right-of-way — the county road or shoulder — which calls for a Prince George's County Use & Occupancy permit. With private-driveway placement so strongly preferred here, most jobs never trigger it, and we will flag it in advance if your address is the rare case that does.

**A note on the bigger jobs.** Beltsville also carries a real commercial pipeline — the Beltsville Business and Industrial Park along Ammendale Road and the Konterra campus near I-95 — with warehouse fit-outs, tenant turnovers, and demolition. Those usually outgrow a 10-yard fast, but they are still work we know well, and we will match you to the right container if a small office or break-room cleanout is all you have.

**Drive time.** We dispatch every morning, and Beltsville is an easy run via I-95 or Route 1. Most addresses here are scheduled within 24 hours, and an early call may still make a same-day run. No other town we serve gets the truck quicker.
