## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Why placement is the defining challenge here.** Silver Spring's near-in neighborhoods are compact and old. Four Corners and South Four Corners are post-WWII subdivisions of Cape Cods, ramblers, and split-levels with short driveways; Kemp Mill is largely 1950s single-family homes; and houses backing onto the Sligo Creek stream valley — which threads past Woodside, Four Corners, and Long Branch — sit in or near floodplain and parkland that constrains where a container can sit and often forces driveway-only placement. A 30-yard needs a long, level run-up and real clearance for the truck, so on these lots we scout the spot with you before delivery and lay boards under the wheels on request.

**The permit rule.** Silver Spring is unincorporated Montgomery County, so permits go through the Department of Permitting Services (MCDPS). A 30-yard that sits entirely on private property — your driveway or a commercial lot — generally needs no special county permit for a short-term placement. But any placement within a county roadway requires a **Public Right-of-Way Permit**, and those carry real strings: the permit expires six months from issuance, can require traffic-control devices like cones and barricades, and on high-volume roads or in business districts can require a full traffic-control plan. State or municipal roadways require a permit from that jurisdiction instead. That is why fitting a roll-off on tight private property is almost always the better play here — dropping it in the street triggers a permit process many quick jobs would rather avoid. If your address is in an HOA, be aware that the association may have its own approval rules on top of the county's, so it is worth checking before we deliver.

**A scheduling note for Kemp Mill.** Kemp Mill is home to one of the largest Orthodox Jewish communities on the East Coast, and Sabbath-observant households often need delivery and pickup scheduled to avoid Friday evening through Saturday. Just tell us when you call and we will plan the run around it.
