## What fits in a 20-yard dumpster

The 20-yard measures 16 ft long by 8 ft wide by 5 ft tall and holds roughly six pickup-truck loads of debris. It comes with a 2-ton (4,000 lb) weight allowance, and a typical Bowie rental runs **$450 to $525, including delivery, pickup, and that tonnage** — no tipping-fee surprises tacked on after the fact.

Those five feet of wall height are the part people underestimate. It is low enough that you can still walk debris up and toss it over the side, but tall enough to swallow the volume of a real project. That balance is why the 20-yard sits in the sweet spot for so many homes here: bigger than a quick-cleanup 10 or 15-yard, but not the oversized 30-yard that mostly makes sense for new construction or full structural demo. It is the size most customers end up choosing — and rarely regret. On a standard two-car driveway, it fits with room to spare.

## Which Bowie jobs suit the 20-yard

This is the size people reach for when the work is real but contained to one home. Around Bowie, that means a few jobs come up again and again:

- **Whole-house cleanouts.** This is the 20-yard's best-known role. Clearing a Belair home between owners, or after years of accumulation, fills a 20-yard about right without paying for space you will not use.
- **Multi-room remodels.** A kitchen-and-two-baths gut, or a run of bedrooms getting new flooring and drywall, is the classic 20-yard job in a Levitt home that is on its second or third renovation.
- **Estate cleanouts.** When a longtime family home in one of the early Belair sections — Somerset, Buckingham, Kenilworth, Foxhill, Tulip Grove — changes hands, the 20-yard handles furniture, boxes, and household debris in a single container.
- **Mid-size roofing tear-offs.** A typical Belair roof tear-off fits comfortably, with the 2-ton allowance covering most single-layer asphalt jobs.

It helps to know that Bowie is not all one kind of house, because that changes what size you actually need. The Levitt sections — Mitchellville and Pointer Ridge among the established neighborhoods — are quarter-acre lots with real driveways and a remodel cycle in full swing, squarely 20-yard territory. Old Town Bowie, the original railroad town, has older and smaller lots on narrower streets, where placement matters more than capacity. And on the newer end, Fairwood is the city's premier planned community, roughly 1,800 upscale single-family homes and townhomes built in phases starting around 2004 to 2005. Those larger homes with bigger footprints can push a gut-and-basement job toward a 30-yard, while the townhome rows have tighter shared driveways. If you tell us your address and the project, we will tell you straight whether the 20 is the right call.

## Local logistics: placement, permits, and drive time

**Driveway placement.** This is where Bowie has a built-in advantage. Belair was laid out on uniform quarter-acre lots — many around 8,400 square feet — with an enclosed garage and a real driveway on every home. That means the dumpster can almost always sit on your own driveway rather than the street. A 20-yard needs roughly 16 feet of straight length plus a few feet of clearance and an open swing for the rear door, and most two-car driveways here handle that easily. We will lay boards under the wheels on request to protect the surface. In Old Town Bowie, where lots are smaller and streets narrower, we will scout placement with you so the truck clears and the container fits.

**The Bowie permit rule.** The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. Driveways and HOA-approved staging do not. Because so many Bowie jobs stage on the homeowner's own driveway, a city street permit is the exception here rather than the rule. If the only practical place is the street, that permit goes through Bowie's Public Works Department at the city offices on Annapolis Road, and we will flag it the moment we look at your address.

**HOAs.** Fairwood and other governed communities often ask for HOA-approved placement before a container drops, and townhome sections in particular have tighter shared driveways and parking to plan around. Give your board a heads-up, and we can provide a service confirmation to forward along.

**Drive time.** We are about 25 minutes out in Beltsville and dispatch every morning by Route 197 or the BW Parkway. Bowie is the largest city in Prince George's County and spread across nearly 19 square miles, so the exact drive depends on your corner of town — but most addresses are scheduled inside 24 hours, and early calls often make the same-day run.
