Landover FAQ

Local questions.

Do I need a permit in Landover?
Landover is unincorporated PG County — county rules apply. Right-of-way placement requires a county permit; private property does not. Landover Hills (the small incorporated town) has its own town permit for street placement.
Do you handle commercial accounts on Route 202?
Yes — net-15 terms are available with approved credit. Multiple weekly swaps are common on active job sites; just call dispatch to set up a recurring schedule.
How early can a Landover dumpster show up?
First-of-day deliveries leave Beltsville at 7 AM. For Landover, that means most morning deliveries land between 7:15 and 8:30.
