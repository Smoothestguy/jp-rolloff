College Park FAQ

Local questions.

Do I need a permit in College Park?
The City of College Park requires a right-of-way permit if the dumpster sits in the street. Permits are issued through the Department of Public Works and we can help you apply. Driveways and private lots are permit-free.
Do you handle UMD student move-outs?
Yes — landlords and property managers reserve us months in advance for the late-April / early-May move-out rush. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks ahead if you need a guaranteed window during that period.
Can you deliver to the Route 1 / Discovery District area?
Yes. We work with several of the contractors building along the Baltimore Avenue corridor. Coordination with the GC and site supervisor is standard — just give us 24h on commercial sites.
