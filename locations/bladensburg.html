¿Atienden en español?
Sí. Llámanos al (301) 252-3586 — la oficina atiende en español y casi todos los conductores también. Puedes enviar tu solicitud por mensaje en español y respondemos igual.
Do I need a permit in Bladensburg?
The Town of Bladensburg requires a permit for dumpsters in the street or right-of-way. Driveway placement is permit-free. The town hall is responsive and turnaround is generally same-week.
Will it fit on a tight Bladensburg lot?
Most older Bladensburg lots accommodate a 10 or 15-yard comfortably. 20-yard placements take careful planning. Send us a photo of your spot and we'll spec it correctly.
