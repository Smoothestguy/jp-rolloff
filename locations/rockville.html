Rockville FAQ

Local questions.

Do I need a permit in Rockville?
The City of Rockville requires a permit for any dumpster placed in a city street or right-of-way. Driveways and private property are permit-free. Rockville's permit office is responsive and turnaround is usually 2–3 business days.
How long does delivery take from Beltsville?
About 45 minutes via I-495 or Route 200. We dispatch Rockville runs early in the morning — so a noon booking generally gets a next-day window, and an 8 AM booking can land same-day.
Do you cover King Farm and the Town Center condos?
Yes. Both are doable with the right size. Town Center condos usually need HOA pre-approval for placement and short rental windows — we work with that constraint.
