Service Area · Upper Marlboro, MD

Dumpster rental in Upper Marlboro, MD.

The PG County seat — and one of the most varied service zones in the DMV. Historic downtown, new construction in Westphalia and Marlton, and large-lot homes throughout 20772 and 20774.

Why Upper Marlboro

Big lots. Big projects. Big trucks welcome.

Upper Marlboro has more space than almost anywhere inside the Beltway. New developments in Westphalia and Marlton mean a constant flow of new-construction debris, and the older rural-residential lots throughout the area regularly need 25 or 30-yard containers for additions and barn cleanouts.

We run trucks down to Upper Marlboro daily and handle commercial accounts at the courthouse complex and the surrounding office parks.

Upper Marlboro FAQ

Local questions.

Do I need a permit in Upper Marlboro?
The Town of Upper Marlboro (the small historic core) has its own permit; the rest of 20772/20774 falls under PG County. Right-of-way placement requires a permit in both cases. Private property typically does not.
Can you deliver to a new-build site in Westphalia?
Yes — we work with several Westphalia builders and developers. Site access constraints (gravel grades, mud, lock-box gates) are handled with a phone call to dispatch.
How far is Upper Marlboro from Beltsville?
About 35 minutes via Route 4 or the Capital Beltway. Morning calls usually get a same-day window; afternoon calls roll over to next-morning.
