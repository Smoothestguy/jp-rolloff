Laurel FAQ

Local questions.

Which county is my address in?
Laurel zip codes cross four counties: 20707 is mostly PG County (City of Laurel), 20708 is PG (South Laurel), 20723 is Howard County (North Laurel), and 20724 is Anne Arundel (Russett / Maryland City). Permitting rules differ by jurisdiction — we'll tell you which one applies based on your address.
Do I need a permit in Laurel?
The City of Laurel requires a permit for any dumpster placed in a public right-of-way. Howard, PG, and Anne Arundel County roads have separate rules. Driveways and private property are typically permit-free. Call us with your address and we'll spell it out.
How fast can you deliver to Russett or Maryland City?
Same-day if you call before noon. Russett and Maryland City are just east of I-95 — quick run from our Beltsville yard.
