Riverdale Park FAQ

Local questions.

Do I need a permit in Riverdale Park?
The Town of Riverdale Park requires a permit for dumpsters in the public right-of-way. Town hall is responsive and turnaround is usually 1–2 business days. Driveways are typically permit-free.
Will a 20-yard fit on a Queensbury bungalow lot?
Usually yes, but it'll take most of the driveway. A 15-yard is a good compromise on the older smaller lots if you want to keep car access.
Can you stage during a Town Center commercial build?
Yes — we work with several of the GCs along East-West Highway. 24-hour advance notice is standard for commercial deliveries.
