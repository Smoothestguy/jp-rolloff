Lanham FAQ

Local questions.

Do I need a permit in Lanham?
Lanham is unincorporated PG County. Right-of-way permits are required for street placement; private driveways are not. We can walk you through the county application if needed.
Can you deliver near NASA Goddard?
Yes — we work with the residential neighborhoods around Goddard regularly. Security-controlled sites inside the federal facility need their own clearance; just give us the access details.
How fast from Beltsville to Lanham?
15 minutes via Route 450 or the BW Parkway. Quick drops most of the day.
