Why Takoma Park

Historic homes. Narrow streets. We've delivered here for years.

Takoma Park is one of the trickier delivery spots in the DMV — protected trees, narrow lanes, short driveways, and a city government that takes its right-of-way rules seriously. We've been navigating it for two decades.

For Old Takoma's tightest blocks (Tulip, Sycamore, Holly), we recommend a 10 or 15-yard. North Takoma's wider streets can take a 20 or 25 comfortably. We'll spec the right size from your address.

Takoma Park FAQ

Local questions.

Do I need a permit in Takoma Park?
Yes, almost always. The City of Takoma Park requires a permit for any dumpster placed in the street or right-of-way, and many blocks ban street placement entirely. The city's permit office is responsive — we'll point you at the application.
What about Takoma Park's tree-protection rules?
Takoma Park enforces its tree canopy rules strictly. If your driveway has overhanging branches, let us know — we'll send a smaller truck or recommend a smaller container to avoid scrapes.
Will my historic-district home need extra approval?
No — dumpster placement on private property doesn't trigger historic-district review. Renovation work might, but that's a separate process handled with your contractor.
