## Roll-Off Service Built for the Jericho Park Corridor, Not the Tract Sections

Most of Bowie was built in one stretch. Between roughly 1960 and 1965, Levitt & Sons stamped about 7,500 Cape Cod, ranch, and Colonial homes across the Belair at Bowie communities — Meadowbrook, Tulip Grove, Kenilworth, Somerset, Buckingham, Heather Hills, and Foxhill. Tight lots, short driveways, near-identical floor plans.

The area around Bowie State University is a different animal entirely. Up on the northern edge of the city in 20715, along the Jericho Park Road and Laurel-Bowie Road corridor, the housing is a low-density mix of mid-century homes, custom builds, and newer contemporary farmhouse-style houses — large two-story craftsman homes with spacious yards and attached front-facing garages. Custom-built houses sit on multi-acre parcels, and the county's own planners describe this stretch as "rural," placing it in the Rural Tier and Developing Tier of the 2006 Master Plan for Bowie and Vicinity. The median home price in the Jericho Park neighborhood was around $735,000 as of December 2024.

That land character changes what a dumpster job looks like up here. Instead of squeezing a can onto a narrow Levitt driveway, we're usually staging on long private drives with acreage to spare. The work skews bigger: estate cleanouts on large lots, custom-home remodels, and land-clearing debris. JP Roll-Off Service is a family-owned hauler, and the 20-yard roll-off is the size we drop most often for these properties.

## What Fits in a 20-Yard Dumpster

The 20-yard is our most popular container because it hits the sweet spot for a full-size home project without the bulk of the 30-yard.

### The numbers

- **Dimensions:** 16 ft long x 8 ft wide x 5 ft high
- **Capacity:** about 6 pickup-truck loads
- **Weight allowance:** 2 tons (4,000 lb) included
- **Typical price:** $450 to $525, including delivery, pickup, and tonnage

The 5-foot wall height matters on a property like the ones up here. It's tall enough to swallow furniture, cabinetry, and bulky construction debris, but low enough that you can still pitch material over the side without a ramp or a lift.

### Bowie State area jobs that suit the 20-yard

- **Whole-house cleanouts.** When a larger Jericho Park or Laurel-Bowie home gets emptied — furniture, carpet, decades of stored belongings — the 20-yard handles a full single-family home in one pull.
- **Estate cleanouts.** The big custom and mid-century homes on multi-acre lots up here generate estate volumes that overwhelm a 10- or 15-yard. The 20-yard is right-sized for clearing a whole household.
- **Multi-room remodels.** Custom-home kitchens, finished basements, and multi-bath renovations produce a steady stream of demo debris. One 20-yard usually carries a multi-room project start to finish.
- **Mid-size roofing tear-offs.** For a single layer of shingles on a larger home, the 20-yard holds the tear-off without you babysitting the weight on a smaller can.

If you're only doing a single bathroom or a garage purge, ask us about a 10- or 15-yard instead — we'd rather right-size the can than oversell you. And if you're clearing a full estate plus outbuildings, or staging for the kind of new construction coming to the area (the planned "Campus Village" mixed-use project on the 93-acre county site by the MARC station, and Bowie State's announced "Gateway" with retail and student housing), the 30-yard may be the better call.

## Local Logistics on the Northern Edge of Bowie

### Driveway placement is the norm here

This is where the Bowie State corridor genuinely differs from central Belair at Bowie. Many Jericho Park and Laurel-Bowie homes have long private driveways and real acreage, so we can almost always set the 20-yard on your own property. That keeps the can off the street, out of the way, and clear of any permit question. We put down boards under the wheels and feet to protect asphalt and pavers, and we position the container so the door end faces your work — whether that's a garage, a side yard, or the back of the house.

On a 16-foot-long box, plan for roughly 22 feet of straight clearance to set and later hook the can, plus about 18 feet of overhead room (mind any low limbs — and given that Belt Woods and the wooded corridor along Jericho Park Road put plenty of mature trees in this area, low branches are worth a look before drop-off).

### When you actually need a permit

The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. If we stage it on your driveway, or on an HOA-approved spot, no city street permit is needed. Because so much of this corridor is large-lot property with room to place on-site, most jobs here never touch the right-of-way at all.

A second rule can apply: placement in the public right-of-way also falls under Prince George's County, where a Site/Road permit through the Department of Permitting, Inspections and Enforcement generally applies to right-of-way placement, while a private driveway typically does not. Given the long private driveways common on the Jericho Park and Laurel-Bowie homes, on-property placement is usually the simple, permit-free path. If your situation does call for the street, tell us when you book and we'll walk you through it.

We dispatch from Beltsville, about 25 minutes from the Bowie State area. That's a short, direct run up to the 20715 corridor, which keeps our delivery and pickup windows tight and lets us swing back quickly when your project wraps and you're ready for the haul-off.

## What's Included and How Pricing Works

Our 20-yard rate of $450 to $525 is an all-in number: delivery to your property, the rental period, pickup, and the first 2 tons (4,000 lb) of disposal. No surprise line items for the basics. Heavier loads — think roofing shingles or anything with a lot of dense material — can run past the 2-ton allowance, so if you're not sure where your debris will land, call and describe the job. We'd rather set expectations honestly up front than hand you a confusing bill later.

A few materials can't go in any roll-off: hazardous waste, wet paint, tires, and similar restricted items. When you book, just tell us what you're throwing out and we'll flag anything that needs to be handled separately.
