## What fits in a 20-yard dumpster?

A 20-yard roll-off measures roughly **16 feet long by 8 feet wide by 5 feet tall** and holds about **6 pickup-truck loads** of debris. It comes with a **2-ton (4,000 lb) weight allowance** included, which covers the mixed household and construction debris most Mitchellville jobs produce.

The five-foot walls matter here. They're low enough that you can walk a wheelbarrow up a ramp or toss bagged debris over the side without a ladder, but the footprint still swallows the volume of a multi-room project. It's the workhorse size — the one we drop most often when a job is too big for a 10 or 15-yard but doesn't need a full 30.

### Mitchellville jobs that suit the 20-yard

Given how Mitchellville's housing stock skews — larger, newer, owner-occupied homes with high incomes and a lot of voluntary upgrading — these are the projects we see the 20-yard handle best:

- **Whole-house cleanouts.** Clearing a 4,000-square-foot colonial before a sale or after a move generates more than most people expect. The 20-yard absorbs furniture, carpet, closets, and garage clutter in one pull.
- **Multi-room remodels.** Kitchen plus a couple of bathrooms, or a main-level refresh. Cabinets, tile, drywall, and flooring add up fast in these larger floor plans.
- **Finished-basement projects.** A Mitchellville staple. Tearing out old finishes or framing out a new lower level produces a steady stream of drywall, lumber, and old paneling that the 20-yard is sized for.
- **Estate cleanouts.** Mitchellville has deep roots and a number of large legacy parcels — including some of the oldest continuously inhabited property in Prince George's County. Estate and downsizing cleanouts on big lots routinely fill a 20-yard.
- **Mid-size roofing tear-offs.** A single-layer tear-off on a typical Mitchellville colonial fits well within the 20-yard's volume and weight allowance.

If you're doing a lighter, single-room job, ask us about the 10 or 15-yard. If you're gutting the whole house to the studs, the 25 or 30-yard may save you a second haul. When in doubt, call and describe the project — we'll tell you honestly which size keeps your cost down.

### Do you need a permit?

For the vast majority of Mitchellville driveway drops, no permit is needed. The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street. If we're placing the can on your own driveway — which we almost always can in Mitchellville, given the lot sizes — no permit is required. HOA-approved staging on your property doesn't need a city permit either, though your subdivision's HOA may want advance notice. If your only option is a public street, let us know and we'll walk you through the right-of-way permit before delivery.
