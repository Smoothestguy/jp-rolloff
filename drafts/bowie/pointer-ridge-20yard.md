## The Levitt homes of Pointer Ridge are hitting renovation age

Pointer Ridge was one of the last single-family sections Levitt and Sons built in the Belair-at-Bowie development, dating to 1968. That makes the neighborhood's housing stock more than 50 years old now — a whole grid of late-1960s and early-1970s Levitt ramblers, ranches, and Colonial Revival homes, including the lower-cost "Kensington" model that filled out these blocks. Half a century is right about when a house starts asking for serious work, and that is exactly what we see here: original owners and second-generation buyers finally gutting the kitchen, tearing out tired bathrooms, pulling a worn-out roof, or clearing a parent's estate.

That wave of renovation debris is specific to Pointer Ridge. Newer Bowie subdivisions are not generating the same volume of plaster, old cabinetry, and worn roofing all at once. The aging Levitt building stock is, and a 20-yard roll-off is the size that handles most of those projects in one drop. We're JP Roll-Off Service — a family-owned hauler — and we run these containers up to Pointer Ridge from our Beltsville yard, about 25 minutes away.

## What a 20-yard dumpster actually holds

The 20-yard is the workhorse of home renovation. The container measures **16 ft long by 8 ft wide by 5 ft tall** and holds about **six pickup-truck loads** of debris. It comes with a **2-ton (4,000 lb) weight allowance**, which is enough headroom for the mixed household and construction debris these projects throw off.

The 5-foot wall height matters on a Pointer Ridge job. It's tall enough to give you real volume for bulky cabinetry, old framing, and furniture, but the walk-in door on the back end still lets you wheel a loaded barrow straight in rather than heaving everything over the top. For a homeowner working a weekend gut, that's the difference between a manageable job and a sore back.

### Pointer Ridge jobs that suit the 20-yard

Given the age and size of homes here — most sitting on roughly a third of an acre — the 20-yard fits the projects we get called for most often:

- **Whole-house cleanout** — clearing a Kensington-model rambler or Colonial from top to bottom, whether you're prepping to sell or just reclaiming the place after decades of accumulation.
- **Multi-room remodel** — the classic Pointer Ridge job: a kitchen plus a bath or two, where the original 1968-era cabinets, flooring, and fixtures all come out together.
- **Estate cleanout** — with typical home values running in the $400,000–$600,000 range and a lot of long-held, owner-occupied houses, estate clearing is common, and a 20-yard handles a full home's worth of belongings and old furniture.
- **Mid-size roofing tear-off** — pulling the shingles off a single-family ranch or rambler. Roofing is heavy, so the 2-ton allowance is sized with this in mind; call us if you're stripping multiple layers and we'll talk through it.

If your project is smaller — a single bathroom or a garage purge — a 10- or 15-yard may save you money. If you're combining a roof tear-off with a full gut, you might want to step up. We'll tell you straight which size fits; we'd rather get it right than oversell you a container.

## Where the dumpster goes in Pointer Ridge

The good news for most of the neighborhood: placement is usually easy. The original Levitt homes in Belair came standard with driveways and garages, and many lots offered an option for a 2-car garage above a certain price point. That means most Pointer Ridge properties have a paved driveway with room to set a 20-yard, and the grassy front lawns, shrubbery, and mature trees that give the streets their character generally leave plenty of clear approach to the driveway.

We always recommend **driveway placement** when it works. It keeps the container on your property, off the street, and close to wherever the debris is coming out. We'll set boards down under the container to protect your driveway surface, and we position it so you keep access to your garage where possible.

A note for the attached and townhome-style units: a meaningful share of homes in the Pointer Ridge / Bel Air Village area are row houses or attached homes, and those come with tighter placement and sometimes shared-driveway constraints. If that's your situation, give us a call before you book and describe the setup — we place containers in tight spots all the time, but it helps to plan the drop in advance rather than figure it out with the truck idling on Pointer Ridge Drive.

### Do you need a permit in Bowie?

For most Pointer Ridge homeowners, no. The **City of Bowie only requires a right-of-way permit if the dumpster sits in a city street.** If we set the container on your **driveway** — or in an **HOA-approved staging area** — no permit is needed. Since the majority of homes here have driveway space, most of our Pointer Ridge drops never touch the permit question at all.

If your only option is the street, you'll need that right-of-way permit before we deliver. Tell us when you call and we'll walk you through it so there are no surprises on delivery day.
