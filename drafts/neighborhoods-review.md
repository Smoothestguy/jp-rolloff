### Kenilworth  (2 flagged)

Homeowners should still confirm their specific association before staging a dumpster.
- [ ] Because Kenilworth's housing stock is now roughly 60+ years old and remarkably uniform in age and model, typical projects here cluster around updating original 1960s features — kitchen and bathroom remodels, replacing aging roofs/windows/HVAC, finishing or gutting basements, garage conversions, and rear additions — all of which generate construction and demolition debris suited to roll-off dumpsters.

### Chapel Forge  (4 flagged)
- [ ] Belair at Bowie streets are named alliteratively by section, so Chapel Forge's spine is Chapel Forge Drive; the neighborhood also contains non-C streets such as Milan Way (home to Chapel Forge Early Childhood Center at 12711 Milan Way) and nearby Chelmont Lane. The named-loop/cul-de-sac layout means trucks navigate residential side streets rather than wide arterials to reach a placement.
- [ ] Chapel Forge is in ZIP code 20715, not 20716 — every verified Chapel Forge Drive property listing and the Chapel Forge Early Childhood Center (12711 Milan Way) carry the 20715 ZIP. The task brief's stated 20716 appears incorrect for this neighborhood.
- [ ] Chapel Forge sits north of Annapolis Road (MD 450) and east of Collington Road, within the City of Bowie in Prince George's County — close to the MD 450 / US 301 / US 50 corridors that give haulers highway access toward Washington, D.C. and Baltimore.
- [ ] The original Levitt sections like Chapel Forge are organized around civic associations (and PTAs) rather than the deed-restricted, dues-collecting HOAs found in newer Bowie subdivisions; the City of Bowie maintains an official homeowners-and-civic-association directory. There is no evidence Chapel Forge is gated, so dumpster placement is governed mainly by the Bowie right-of-way rule plus any voluntary civic-association courtesy norms, not gate access or HOA staging approval.

### Whitehall  (5 flagged)

- [ ] A very common Levitt-Bowie renovation is converting the original carport/attached open space into finished living area, finishing attics, and opening up galley kitchens — the kind of mid-century-home remodels that generate framing, drywall, and old-finish debris well suited to a 10-15 yard dumpster.
- [ ] Belair (and thus Whitehall) sits on land that was part of the historic 500-acre Belair estate/Belair Mansion; Whitehall is an established, non-gated suburban Levitt section rather than a gated or amenity-controlled community.
- [ ] NeighborWho lists Whitehall Drive in Bowie under ZIP 20715 (with ~58 addresses), not 20716 as stated in the task brief — the ZIP code for the Whitehall section should be verified before publishing, as Bowie spans both 20715 and 20716.
- [ ] The City of Bowie maintains an official HOA/Civic Association listing; a Whitehall homeowners/civic association is referenced for the section, but specific dues/membership terms could not be reliably verified (a 'whitehallhoa.org' site found in search actually belongs to an unrelated Whitehall in Irmo, South Carolina). Treat any HOA-specific staging rules as 'check with the local association.'

### Overbrook  (3 flagged)

- [ ] Idlewild Drive alone contains roughly 90+ addresses with an average build year around 1965, indicating a dense, fully-built-out section of detached homes on uniform Levitt-era lots -- the kind of mature suburban lots where 10-20 yard dumpsters fit comfortably on driveways for roofing, garage/basement cleanouts and single-room remodels.

### Old Town Bowie  (2 flagged)
- [ ] Old Town Bowie is covered by an Old Town Bowie Development District Overlay Zone (DDOZ) administered through Prince George's County / M-NCPPC planning, which applies tailored development and design review standards to the historic district. Projects altering structures here can fall under added review beyond standard Bowie zoning.
- [ ] A city-backed revitalization of Old Town Bowie (funded in 2024, construction expected to begin in 2025) plans to add art galleries, a brewery, and a performing arts venue, signaling near-term commercial buildout and tenant-improvement/cleanout activity in the district.

### Mitchellville  (2 flagged)
- [ ] Multiple HOAs operate in and around Mitchellville/Woodmore (the area reportedly had about seven area homeowner associations tied to the Woodmore golf community, plus nearby Lake Arbor HOA). HOA-governed communities commonly require approved staging locations and gate access coordination for dumpsters, which matters more here than in non-HOA parts of Bowie.
- [ ] Mitchellville (and the adjacent Woodmore CDP) falls within Prince George's County's larger-lot residential zoning context, where estate-type zones permit lots of roughly one acre or larger and R-R zoning permits about half-acre lots. This is consistent with the 0.5-1 acre lots typical of the neighborhood, giving ample room for on-site dumpster placement on driveways.

- [ ] PERMIT RULE (provided, use as-is — but note jurisdiction caveat): The instruction states 'The City of Bowie requires a right-of-way permit only if the dumpster sits in a city street; driveways and HOA-approved staging do not.'
