About

Two decades. One family. The DMV.

We started JP Roll-Off Service in 2005 with one truck and a simple promise: answer the phone, show up on time, charge what we quoted. Twenty years later, that's still the whole pitch.

Our story

A family business, built call by call.

JP Roll-Off Service was founded in Beltsville and we've never left. We grew because contractors and homeowners kept calling us back — not because of a flashy ad, but because the dumpster showed up when we said it would. After 5,000+ loads, that's still our edge.

We're bilingual (English & Spanish), we know every backroad in PG County, and our drivers care where they place your container. If you've used the big national haulers, you'll feel the difference on the first call.
